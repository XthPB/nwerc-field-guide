Digit DP, binary bounds and carries

Follow a prefix that still equals the bound and one that is already smaller. Then add the smallest useful memory for a zero, pattern, residue, carry or second number.

  • Translate an enormous numeric interval into a short stringCount integers from 10 to 25 with at most one nonzero digit, without looping over every integer in a large interval.
  • Make leading zeros and the actual number zero behave correctlyHow many integers from 0 through 20 contain a zero in their ordinary decimal representation?
  • Handle a bound with ten thousand decimal digitsCount positive integers at most a huge decimal string K whose digit sum is divisible by D. How do you avoid parsing K into an integer?
  • Attach a residue, digit mask, position, or automatonCount numbers divisible by every nonzero digit they contain. What should survive after reading a prefix?
  • Digit products can return from above the limitCount positive integers whose digit product is at most 5. Can you discard prefix 9 immediately?
  • Count bounded numbers avoiding a digit patternCount two-digit numbers from 10 through 25 that avoid the substring 12. What must the digit DP remember about the current suffix?
  • Sum digits, sum values, or compute moments without a sum axisYou know there are two prefixes, 1 and 3, with total value 4. What happens to their combined value if both append digit 5?
  • Choose and order actual digitsRearrange digits 1,1,2 into different valid numbers. How do you keep the identical copies from doubling the answer?
  • Construct the k-th number satisfying a digit ruleFind the fourth positive integer up to 30 with strictly increasing decimal digits. Can you skip whole groups of candidates?
  • Change direction for arithmetic carriesHow many ordered pairs of nonnegative two-bit numbers sum to binary 10?
  • Count bounded pairs whose binary addition has no carryHow many ordered pairs (a,b), each between 0 and 3, satisfy a+b = a XOR b?
  • Classify a large binary number by its popcountHow many positive integers up to binary N need exactly k applications of popcount to reach 1?

Test your recognition
Count numbers containing a real digit zero in [1,N]. Would ordinary fixed-width leading zeros work?

Keep started and seenRealZero flags. Padding before the first nonzero digit leaves both unchanged. Handle the actual integer zero separately if it enters the requested interval.

You want the sum of qualifying numbers, not a histogram of their values. What extra information does each machine state need?

A count and a sum. Appending digit d in base b sends (count,sum) to (count,b·sum+d·count).
