Digits, bounds and carries

Is 007 a three-digit object, a representation of 7, or a forbidden prefix? The answer changes the automaton.

  • Translate an enormous numeric interval into a short stringCount integers from 10 to 25 with at most one nonzero digit, without looping over every integer in a large interval.
  • Attach a residue, digit mask, position, or automatonCount numbers divisible by every nonzero digit they contain. What should survive after reading a prefix?
  • Change direction for arithmetic carriesHow many ordered pairs of nonnegative two-bit numbers sum to binary 10?
  • Choose and order actual digitsRearrange digits 1,1,2 into different valid numbers. How do you keep the identical copies from doubling the answer?
  • Sum digits, sum values, or compute moments without a sum axisYou know there are two prefixes, 1 and 3, with total value 4. What happens to their combined value if both append digit 5?

Test your recognition
Count numbers containing a real digit zero in [1,N]. Would ordinary fixed-width leading zeros work?

Keep started and seenRealZero flags. Padding before the first nonzero digit leaves both unchanged. Handle the actual integer zero separately if it enters the requested interval.

You want the sum of qualifying numbers, not a histogram of their values. What extra information does each machine state need?

A count and a sum. Appending digit d in base b sends (count,sum) to (count,b·sum+d·count).
