Constructive proofs and implementation · BAPC 2024-A

“Aaawww...” or “Aaayyy!!!”

You know the ranking when a contest scoreboard froze. You then hear the crowd as pending submissions are revealed. The chants say whether a submission succeeded and how many places its team gained. Where does your favorite team finish?

The observation

You do not need submission times or score tie-breakers: the input already tells you every rank movement. Preserve team identity while rearranging the ranking.

Why it works

At each step the rules uniquely identify the lowest-ranked team with a pending submission. Decrement its pending count and move it up by the reported amount. Inductively, the maintained order equals the real order after every reveal. Leftmost pending position is irrelevant to rank evolution once only the count and the chant matter.

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Simulate every scoreboard cell

Keep the whole matrix, find the next pending cell by scanning the ranking and row, and rotate the corresponding team after acceptance. The small limits make this feasible.

Valid baseline · O(n²m²) loose bound time · O(nm) space

Keep pending counts and an order vector

Replace each row by its number of pending submissions. Find the last ranked team with count>0 and move it with adjacent swaps. There are at most nm events, each costing O(n).

Recommended · O(n²m) time · O(n) space

Linked-list rank maintenance

Keep team nodes in a list and splice the resolved node before its new predecessor. Moving by j ranks still costs O(j) because the target must be found; a list does not magically make rank lookup constant.

Alternative representation · O(n²m) time · O(n) space

Track the favourite through each move

You still need the ranking to find the active team, but you can update the favourite’s rank directly: if it moves, subtract jump; if another team crosses it, add one. This is a useful independent cross-check.

Alternative state reduction · O(n²m) time · O(n) space

Implicit treap with pending-count augmentation

Store subtree size and whether any pending result remains. Find the rightmost pending node, split it out by rank, and reinsert at rank−jump. This is appropriate only for much larger n; learn it after the vector version.

Advanced generalization · Expected O(nm log n) time · O(n) space

Boundary checks

Count extra y characters beyond the three in Aaayyy!!!. A rejected submission still consumes one pending result. The favourite is an immutable team ID, not a mutable rank.
