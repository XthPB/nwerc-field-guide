Probability, expectation and randomization · BAPC 2021-E

Evolutionary Excerpt

Produce a common subsequence of length at least 500000 from two independent uniformly random DNA strings of length 10^6.

The observation

You do not need the longest common subsequence. Local LCS on corresponding short blocks, or a narrow diagonal band, already finds enough matches with very high probability under the specified distribution.

Why it works

For each pair of corresponding blocks, an LCS is a subsequence of both blocks. Concatenating the block results preserves order globally, so validity is deterministic. Reaching half the total length is probabilistic and depends on the random-string promise; arbitrary strings need not have any common character.

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Full LCS

The standard recurrence is the right starting model but n=10^6 makes it impossible.

Exact but infeasible · O(n²) time · O(n²) with reconstruction space

Match the front greedily

Take equal front characters; otherwise advance one side using a simple balancing rule. The editorial reports about 0.4n matches, below the required half.

Insufficient expected output · O(n) time · O(n) output space

Short lookahead greedy

Inspect the next few characters on both sides and choose a nearby match. Lookahead 3–4 improves the empirical yield, but the policy and distribution analysis matter.

Probabilistic alternative · O(nk) for fixed lookahead k time · O(n) output space

LCS inside a diagonal band

Keep cells with |i−j|≤k. The resulting path is a valid common subsequence and empirically long enough; unlike full LCS, it may exclude the global optimum.

Advanced probabilistic alternative · O(nk) time · O(nk) straightforward reconstruction space

Independent short-block LCS

Solve LCS exactly for each aligned pair of length-10 blocks and concatenate. The jury provides this approach. The margin above n/2 is substantial for the promised random input, while implementation stays small.

Recommended simple probabilistic solution · O(nk), e.g. k=10 time · O(k²+n) including output space

Boundary checks

Separate subsequence validity from meeting the target length. The usual front-character greedy is too weak on average. Reconstruct the characters, not just the DP length. Do not advertise a probabilistic length guarantee as an adversarial guarantee.
