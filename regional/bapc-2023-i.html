Range queries and sweeps · BAPC 2023-I

International Irregularities

Each country has an infection rate and a quarantine duration. A trip between two countries takes a day, with quarantine at the destination if the rate falls by more than a given threshold. For many start and end pairs, find the fastest journey, allowing stops along the way.

The observation

An optimal route either uses only free-of-quarantine hops, or pays quarantine once at the beginning. Greedy free hops can be accelerated with binary lifting.

Why it works

If a route pays quarantine after some earlier travel, jump directly to that quarantined destination and remove the earlier prefix: the direct trip costs at most the same quarantine charge, and saves travel days. Repeating this removes all but the last paid quarantine. Thus compare a pure-hop route from x with a source-independent best paid-entry route to y. For decreasing rates, the farthest legal free hop is always optimal because it makes maximal progress without harming future reachability. Precompute these successors and their powers of two. The best paid-entry costs can be computed by a monotone priority-queue sweep over destinations, as in the included jury implementation.

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Build the complete weighted graph

Every pair of countries has a directed edge with the literal travel cost. APSP gives an independent exact oracle for testing structural optimizations.

Small-input oracle · O(n³+q) with Floyd–Warshall time · O(n²) space

Dijkstra separately for each query

Generate outgoing costs on demand rather than storing all edges. This saves memory, not enough time.

Exact but too slow · O(qn²) dense implementation time · O(n) space

One quarantine plus explicit hop chains

After the structural proof, compute best[y]=min_z(1+t_z+freeHopDistance(z,y)). Answer with min(best[y],freeHopDistance(x,y)). Enumerating all entry points still costs quadratic time.

Intermediate exact model · O(n²) preprocessing; O(n) per query time · O(n) space

Binary lifting for the pure-hop route

Two pointers find the lowest-rate country reachable without quarantine from each country. Doubling stores 2^b greedy hops. Test fixed points and count hops by descending powers.

Essential acceleration · O(n log n) build; O(log n) per hop query time · O(n log n) space

Priority sweep plus binary lifting

Initialize entry cost B[i]=1+t_i and a min-heap containing all such options. Sweep rates high to low. Discard heap entries whose rate is more than m above the current destination; the remaining minimum can reach it in one free hop. Relax B[i] by that minimum+1 and insert the improved value. Lower-rate initial entries capture jumping past a target; already processed higher entries capture chains of downward free hops.

Recommended advanced method · O((n+q) log n) time · O(n log n) space

Boundary checks

If m=0 or repeated rates create a successor fixed point, detect unreachable pure-hop destinations. Upward/equal-rate travel costs one day. The preprocessing of paid-entry routes is essential; min(1+t_y, pure hops) alone misses cheaper quarantine in another country.
