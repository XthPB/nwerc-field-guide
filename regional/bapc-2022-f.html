Strings and pattern matching · BAPC 2022-F

Failing Flagship

Decode two compass strings of length up to 1000 and report their smallest angular separation.

The observation

Within a quadrant, the encoding is dyadic. Start at its two-letter diagonal, then fold the preceding letters from LEFT to RIGHT by averaging with the indicated cardinal endpoint. Unwrap NW as the interval [270°,360°].

Why it works

Each additional binary choice refines the quadrant’s angular grid. The fold encodes the choices with successively halved weights; the most recently folded character receives weight 1/2. Working in an unwrapped interval ensures every average lies on the correct short arc. Finally reduce the difference modulo 360 and choose min(d,360−d).

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Build the whole compass refinement

Explicitly generate every direction at each resolution and locate the string. This makes the nested dyadic grid visible but is impossible for long strings.

Exponential baseline · O(2^L) time · O(2^L) space

Exact dyadic arithmetic

Represent a position inside its quadrant by an integer numerator over a power of two. Boost cpp_int avoids overflow; converting the final fraction to floating point is extra work that the tolerance does not need.

Exact alternative · O(L²) bit operations time · O(L) bits space

Recursive affine fold

Each fold is the affine map z↦(z+endpoint)/2. Applying the prefix in input order gives the correct composition; recursion can express the composition but adds stack depth.

Correct conceptual model · O(L) time · O(L) stack space

Iterative unwrapped angles

Choose low and high cardinal angles for the final diagonal, with N represented as 360 for NW. Apply the fold, then take circular distance.

Recommended · O(L) time · O(1) space

Bound the influence of old digits

After K folds any error in the starting angle shrinks by 2^K. Keeping only the last 40 prefix characters changes the result by at most 90/2^40 degrees. The input still takes O(L) time to read; this is an error-bound lesson, not a meaningful contest speed-up.

Error-analysis extension · O(min(L,K)) arithmetic after reading time · O(1) space

Boundary checks

The string’s recursive wording does not mean repeatedly averaging the suffix with the first cardinal in reverse order. For NW, averaging 0 and 270 directly gives the wrong quadrant. With 1000 letters, floating-point rounding is harmless at 10^−6 absolute accuracy if wraparound is handled correctly.
