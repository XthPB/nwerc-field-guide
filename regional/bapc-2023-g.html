Geometry and numerical methods · BAPC 2023-G

Geometry Game

You’re given the four corners of a quadrilateral in boundary order. Decide the most specific shape that fits, such as a square, rectangle, rhombus, or kite. Some shapes satisfy several definitions, so the order in which you identify them matters.

The observation

Use exact squared lengths, dot products, and cross products. Classification is a hierarchy of predicates, so test stricter classes first.

Why it works

For vectors u,v, u·v=0 characterizes perpendicularity and u×v=0 characterizes parallelism. Squared length u·u preserves length equality without square roots. Evaluate the defining predicates of each shape. A square satisfies both rectangle and rhombus conditions, so returning a broad class first would lose required information.

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Compute lengths and angles with floating point

A direct geometric implementation using sqrt and acos is intuitive but introduces rounding and unnecessary transcendental functions. Near-equal long sides are a classic failure.

Fragile baseline · O(1) time · O(1) space

Exact side-length classification

Compare four squared side lengths and two squared diagonals. Equal sides plus equal diagonals identify a square under the statement’s polygon conditions.

Efficient alternative · O(1) time · O(1) space

Vector predicates and a decision tree

Compute opposite-side parallelism, adjacent perpendicularity, and equal sides. Return the strongest matching type in the required order.

Recommended · O(1) time · O(1) space

Diagonal characterization

Parallelogram diagonals share a midpoint; a rectangle additionally has equal diagonals, and a rhombus has perpendicular diagonals. Express midpoint equality as sums of coordinates to avoid division.

Independent cross-check · O(1) time · O(1) space

Reusable exact geometry kernel

Create robust vector primitives and property-test them under translation, integer scaling, and cyclic relabelling. These transformations preserve the shape and expose overflow or indexing errors.

Advanced engineering · O(1) per predicate time · O(1) space

Boundary checks

The original points are supplied in boundary order; preserve it. A trapezium needs at least one pair of opposite sides parallel, after more restrictive classes have been ruled out. Use wide intermediates for products and avoid comparing floating-point square roots.
