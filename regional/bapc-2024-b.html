Graphs, connectivity and flow · BAPC 2024-B

Buggy Blinkers

You drive along equal-length roads with a limited number of turn-signal activations. Consecutive turns in the same direction can share a signal, going straight needs the signal off, and U-turns are forbidden. Find the shortest legal route to your destination.

The observation

A location is not a complete state. Future legality also depends on arrival direction, remaining activation budget, and current signal state.

Why it works

Build states (intersection, incoming compass slot, activations used, signal state). A road transition determines whether the movement is left, straight, or right. Straight resets the signal; a turn spends one activation exactly when its required signal differs from the current state. Every legal route maps to a path in this state graph and vice versa, with one unit cost per road. BFS finds its shortest path.

From a small instance to the contest constraints

Enumerate legal routes

Search routes while explicitly simulating signal changes. A length cap is needed because cycles exist; useful for tiny differential tests.

Tiny graph oracle · Exponential in route length time · O(route length) space

BFS on intersections only

This tempting shortcut merges states that have different budgets or signals. A shorter arrival may spend all activations, while a longer arrival can still finish. Use this failure to understand why state design precedes algorithm choice.

Incorrect; diagnostic · O(n+m) time · O(n) space

Dijkstra on the expanded state graph

The fully expanded state model is correct with Dijkstra. It is also ready for a variant with unequal nonnegative road lengths.

Correct alternative · O(nk log(nk)) time · O(nk) space

BFS on the expanded state graph

All road costs are one, so replace the heap with a FIFO queue. There are at most n·4·(k+1)·3 states and a constant number of outgoing choices each.

Recommended · O(nk) time · O(nk) space

Pareto dominance within direction/signal

An arrival with no greater distance and no more activations dominates another arrival with the same location, incoming direction, and signal. Keeping only nondominated labels can save work, but needs both inequalities; lower distance alone is insufficient.

Advanced pruning · O(nk) states; implementation-dependent overhead time · O(nk) space

Boundary checks

The compass direction at the other end of a road need not be the geometric opposite of the departure slot: inspect the neighbour’s reciprocal entry. Starting direction is free. Changing left to right is a fresh activation. If start equals destination the answer is zero.
