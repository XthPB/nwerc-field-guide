Knapsack and subset sum

Can I make 5? How many ways make 5? Which two items make 5? All three questions use the same input.

  • Which sums can you make using each item once?Can one weight of 3 fill a bag to exactly 6 if you may use it only once?
  • When the capacity is huge, index by profitA bag holds 7. Three items have weight and profit (8,3), (3,2), (4,2). What profit fits?
  • Identical weights, distinguishable items, and multiplicityThree tickets A, B and C each weigh 2. How many pairs reach weight 4?
  • Update many reachable sums with one shiftWhich totals can you make with weights 2, 3 and 3, using each once?
  • Reuse, bounded stock, or one choice per groupA shop lets you choose one of two upgrades from the same group. Can you take both to get a higher profit?
  • Remove a large multiplicity with a residue-class dequeYou can buy at most two copies of an item weighing 2. How do you update every capacity without trying all copy counts?
  • Cardinality, two budgets, and resource couplingCan you reach exactly 5 with exactly two items? What if the second requirement is a budget for a different resource?
  • Negative weights, balance, residues, and nonempty subsetsCan you choose at least one of +5, −3 and −2 and finish with balance zero?
  • Which voting groups need every member?Three parties have 4, 3 and 2 seats. A group needs 5 of the 9 seats to win. How many winning groups need every one of their members?
  • Partition, complements, gcd, and smallest missing sumWhich positive total is the first you cannot pay with coins 1, 2, 2 and 7?
  • Retrieve the selected items from a compressed sum tableA bitset tells you that weight 5 is reachable. Which of the items 2, 3 and 3 should you actually hand over?
  • Can the cashier make an unsplittable payout?Can a cashier pay 12 cents in coins that two people cannot split equally? The cashier gets to choose the coins.
  • Solve a small geometric instance first, then knapsack its cost curveOn a 2 by 3 board, filling a whole row or column earns a point. What is the cheapest way to earn two points when a painted cell costs one?

Test your recognition
You now need the number of ways to use six identical tickets of value 2. Can you replace the stock with 1,2,3-ticket bundles and count 0/1 subsets?

No. Three tickets are represented by {3} and by {1,2}, although both represent one original choice. Count a bounded type using an old-row sum along its residue class.

A 10⁹ budget comes with 200 items, each earning at most 50 points. How would you recover the optimal score?

Index exact score up to 10000, minimizing weight per score. Scan the scores from high to low for one whose minimum weight fits.
