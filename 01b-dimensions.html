Multidimensional states, from 2D to 4D

You have three strings, aa, b and c, and a host text abac. Can you fit all three into distinct host positions while preserving each string’s order? Each cell of the diagram describes how far you have read in each string.

Start this topic in reading order → · See the whole reading path

  • Build a state by asking which histories can have different futuresYou’re given items of weights 2, 3 and 5. Choose exactly two distinct items whose total weight is exactly 5. A single item of weight 5 satisfies only one of the two requirements. Decide if any selection satisfies both.
  • Two independently advancing strings make a 2D gridYou have source strings ab and c and a target acb. Build the target one letter at a time by taking the next unused letter from either source. Each source must retain its internal order and both must be used up. Decide whether the target can be built.
  • Delete a coordinate using a conservation lawYou must put every item into one of three bags. Each item has a known weight and cannot be split. You want to know which final loads are achievable. After placing weights 2 and 3, for instance, what can you infer if the first two bags contain 2 and 0?
  • Saturate, sparsify or pack a large product of dimensionsEach bundle contains a known number of red and blue tokens. You may take each bundle once. What is the fewest bundles that give you at least 3 red and 2 blue tokens? One available bundle contains 9 red and 1 blue, so going over a target is possible.
  • Fit three growing strings inside a hostYou have a fixed host string abac and three strings aa, b and c. You can interleave the three strings while preserving the order within each. Decide whether some interleaving appears in the host in order, allowing skipped host letters.
  • Three independently depleted piles give three prefix indicesYou have three piles of positive numbers, one for each color. A move removes two numbers of different colors and earns their product. Each number can be used once. Find the largest score, starting with red {7,3}, blue {5} and green {4}.
  • Four boundaries identify an arbitrary subrectangleSome cells of a board are marked. You may paint an axis-aligned rectangle for a cost equal to its longer side, and every marked cell must be covered. On a 3 × 3 board with opposite corners marked, compare covering both together with covering them separately.
  • Pack many small capped resources into one array indexYou can buy projects, each with a price and known increases to several skill scores. Reach at least 2 in each of three skills for the lowest possible cost. Extra skill above the target is harmless. A score of (2,0,1) and a score of (0,2,1) leave different work to do.

Test your recognition
A three-bag allocation tracks the widths of the first two bags. When is it sound to infer the third?

Only when the processed total is known and every processed item has been assigned exactly once. If skipping is allowed, track the total assigned as well or choose a different state.

Three strings grow and shrink while being interleaved in a fixed host. What must be recomputed after popping x and appending y?

Overwrite the newly exposed whole face, in dependency order, with the transitions for the new character. Earlier positions in the host dominate later positions for the same triple.
