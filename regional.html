Learning from regional contests

Across the five completed NWERC editions from 2021 through 2025, the jury presentations all use ordering, graph reasoning and geometry. These recurring ideas guide the reading order. The years 2022 and 2023 are included here only in the five-year topic finding.

What to study after DP

Begin with greedy exchange and sorting, then graph and tree models. Learn geometric predicates early enough to practice using them under time pressure. Follow with offline sweeps, range queries and binary search, then counting, number theory, probability and strings. Constructive proofs and implementation deserve practice alongside each subject. This is a training order inferred from the sets.

Connections across NWERC problems

  • Order books and shelves once, then test a proposed number of reduced-capacity shelves by greedy matching. NWERC 2024 · Limited Library · Turn an optimization target into a decision
  • A pair changes angular order between two viewpoints. Separate the geometric cases and count order inversions. NWERC 2021 · Flatland Olympics · Count intersections by watching an ordering change
  • Remove a group of equal-height entries and query the live positions of the remaining entries with a Fenwick tree. NWERC 2021 · Exchange Students · Find the first position passing a range predicate
  • Use the probability that a uniform point lies in a triangle. Bilinearity of the cross product aggregates triangle areas in boundary order. NWERC 2024 · Mouse Trap · Turn a polygon walk into signed area and lattice counts
  • Give each triangle one indicator. Its success probability is the triangle’s share of polygon area. NWERC 2024 · Mouse Trap · Find the expectation without the whole distribution
  • Order stores by route length, then multiply a store’s availability by the probability that every earlier store is unavailable. The regional task uses fixed stores rather than repeated trials. NWERC 2024 · Kruidnoten · Aggregate retries while admitting destinations in cost order
  • Combine reachable components with permutation parity to test whether the target arrangement can be achieved. NWERC 2024 · Glued Grid · Find a quantity every allowed move preserves
  • Start with a bounding cuboid, then use noninterfering tetrahedral cuts to realize the required volume. NWERC 2021 · Cutting Edge · Construct numbers from an invariant, then check every requirement
  • Each tree edge induces a cut. Aggregating parity across that cut determines which tree edges the tour needs. NWERC 2025 · Canal Crossing · Count many paths by marking only their boundaries
  • Only linearly many special events affect the grid route. An increasing chain or ordered range query replaces the dense grid table. NWERC 2025 · Group Photo · Use an ordering constraint to reduce a two-dimensional choice
  • Periodicity reduces to a gcd condition. Among eligible steps seek the coprime step nearest half the circumference. NWERC 2025 · Dreamcatcher · Move a divisibility condition onto prime exponents
  • Enumerate line intersections with a sweep and organize candidate orientations. Here the sweep supplies candidates rather than a union area. NWERC 2025 · Illuminated Stalls · Sweep slabs and maintain covered length with multiplicity
  • First determine who must carry a key from arrivals to an empty apartment. Check the stock of available keys in a second event sweep. NWERC 2025 · Juggling Keys · Move a boundary only after proving what can be discarded
