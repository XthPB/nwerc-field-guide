Try the idea on a regional problem

Cactus Connectivity. Color a spanning forest of a cactus alternately. Each odd cycle forces one uncut edge, and different cactus cycles share no edge. Every even cycle can keep all its edges cut. A triangle attached to a four-cycle at a common vertex therefore contributes 2+4 cut edges. The jury shows how maximum cut also gives the requested connectivity threshold, one larger. Compare the edge-deletion parity lesson. 2025 ICPC Asia Pacific jury analysis

Tower of Hanoi. A block summary has three entries, one per target rod. Each gives a target for all smaller disks and a move cost. If a disk is already on its target, keep that target for the smaller disks. Otherwise gather the smaller disks on the third rod. Moving this disk and then the tower of s smaller disks costs 2ˢ moves after they have gathered. For two disks starting on A and ending on B, the larger disk contributes 2 and the smaller disk contributes 1. To combine a block of small disks with a block of large disks, shift the large block’s cost by the number of small disks (multiply by the corresponding power of two), then add the small block’s cost at the required rod. This gives an associative range summary. Compare the range-summary lesson. 2025 ICPC Asia Pacific jury analysis
