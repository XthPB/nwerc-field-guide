Regional contest problems

Each example links the contest source to a transferable idea and the code used to implement it.

NWERC

  • 1995 · Block Voting

    Statement-derived comparison with 2024 D. Here one specified party must swing a vote. Count sums of all other parties in its pivotal window. A single all-party count polynomial can be divided by the selected party’s factor to recover that row.

    Read the lesson · 0/1 knapsack
  • 1996 · Dividing coins

    Statement-derived. Two piles always have totals s and S−s. A closest-to-half reachable sum solves a fairness objective with one Boolean axis.

    Read the lesson · Bitset subset sum
  • 1997 · Mendel

    Statement-derived. A tiny probability vector at each tree node summarizes the possible outcomes below it. Combine independent parental contributions at the next node.

    Read the lesson · First-step expectation and probability · Two-state DP with rolling layers
  • 1998 · Numbers & Letters

    Statement-derived. For each small mask store the integers obtainable by expressions on exactly those leaves. Split by the root operator and test both operand orders where relevant, including exact division.

    Read the lesson · Enumerate submasks
  • 1999 · Chess x Tetris

    Statement-derived modelling exercise. Filling the first uncovered square canonically avoids generating all placement orders. A memoized frontier also needs the remaining stock of each shape.

    Read the lesson · Enumerate submasks
  • 2000 · Railroads

    Statement-derived. Among connections reaching the same dated station event, the later initial departure dominates for the stated tie-break. Event time supplies an order, with simultaneous transfers handled consistently.

    Read the lesson · Meet in the middle for profit
  • 2001 · Number Game

    Statement-derived. The legal set lies in a small fixed universe. Memoize winning and losing positions by availability, updating all newly forbidden numbers after each move.

    Read the lesson · Grundy values on a finite game
  • 2002 · Euro Efficiency

    Statement-derived. Giving change permits negative as well as positive coin moves in the net amount. A one-directional unlimited-coin recurrence would omit cheaper overpayment-and-change combinations.

    Read the lesson · Dijkstra on an expanded graph · Two-state DP with rolling layers
  • 2003 · Bridging signals

    Statement-derived reduction. After ordering one shore, two links cross exactly when their endpoint order reverses. The largest noncrossing collection is an LIS. A geometric picture hides a sequence state.

    Read the lesson · Strict longest increasing subsequence
  • 2004 · Minimax Triangulation

    Fix a boundary chord and the third vertex of its triangle. Combine the two remaining subpolygons by their worst triangle, minimizing over valid pivots. A nonconvex polygon also needs a validity test.

    Read the lesson · Interval DP in increasing length · Knuth-optimized adjacent merging
  • 2005 · Declaration of Content

    Statement-derived. A total of 100 and an ordering of ingredient percentages give small states for the previous percentage and remaining total. Prefix/suffix feasibility can bound the possible amount of a specified ingredient.

    Read the lesson · Bitset subset sum · 0/1 knapsack
  • 2006 · The Bookcase

    Statement-derived. Process in descending height so opening a shelf fixes its maximum height. Two accumulated shelf widths determine the third from the processed total. Track how many shelves have opened and minimize paid heights for identical widths.

    Read the lesson · Bitset subset sum · 0/1 knapsack
  • 2007 · Obfuscation

    Statement-derived. Dictionary words with equivalent first/last letters and interior counts can share a match signature. A prefix DP can saturate the number of decodings at two when only impossible, unique, or ambiguous matters.

    Read the lesson · Trie of strings · Topological order and DAG DP
  • 2008 · Easy Climb

    The allowed height change gives each current height a window of predecessor heights. The jury first restricts the candidate heights, then maintains minima as the window moves.

    Read the lesson · Monotone stack and sliding deque
  • 2009 · Mountain Road

    Separate two ordered streams. Their consumed prefix lengths determine the remaining work, but the direction of the last car affects the timing of the next batch.

    Read the lesson · Two-state DP with rolling layers
  • 2010 · Free Goodies

    Sort by one player’s preferences first. A DP by items already allocated and number taken by the other player then becomes possible. The jury also gives a faster greedy route.

    Read the lesson · 0/1 knapsack
  • 2011 · Tichu

    Thirteen cards fit a mask. Precompute legal plays for every subset and partition a remaining hand into as few legal subsets as possible.

    Read the lesson · Enumerate submasks
  • 2012 · Beer Pressure

    Aggregate by a vector of current counts, propagate probability on the resulting lattice, then sum terminal outcomes. Distinct histories with the same counts have the same future law.

    Read the lesson · First-step expectation and probability · Two-state DP with rolling layers
  • 2013 · Jingle Balls

    At a given tree depth, a balanced target distributes totals by floor or ceiling division. Those two possibilities replace an unconstrained count axis in the recursive subproblems.

    Read the lesson · Edit distance
  • 2014 · Cent Savings

    The cost of a rounded group is local once its endpoints are fixed. A last cut gives the baseline prefix DP. Rounding depends on a small residue, allowing a further compression.

    Read the lesson · Divide-and-conquer partition DP · Prefix sums and difference arrays
  • 2015 · Debugging

    Balanced splitting recurses on a rounded quotient of the size. Memoize the resulting sizes instead of building an independent search tree for every number of splits.

    Read the lesson · Edit distance
  • 2016 · Arranging Hat

    The jury’s alternative indexes prefix length and edit budget, keeping the smallest possible final number. That representative leaves the most room for later nondecreasing numbers.

    Read the lesson · Meet in the middle for profit
  • 2017 · Installing Apps

    The temporary download and permanent footprint create an exchange order. In that order, store the least occupied space that attains each number of installed apps and reconstruct the picks.

    Read the lesson · 0/1 knapsack
  • 2017 · English Restaurant

    The last occupied table separates left and right intervals. When averaging over orders, include the number of interleavings of their earlier occupancies.

    Read the lesson · Interval DP in increasing length
  • 2018 · Fastest Speedrun

    Preprocessing exposes functional cycles and removes mandatory costs. Only after that reduction does a DP or shortest-path calculation capture the remaining choices.

    Read the lesson · Dijkstra on an expanded graph · Two-state DP with rolling layers
  • 2019 · Disposable Switches

    Keep the cheapest base length for each exact edge count. A parameter adds the same surcharge per edge, making each edge count a candidate line.

    Read the lesson · Matrix multiplication and power
  • 2020 · Great Expectations

    A restart refers back to the unknown starting expectation. The jury fixes a trial value, evaluates easier downstream states and uses consistency at the start to guide a search.

    Read the lesson · Gaussian elimination · First-step expectation and probability
  • 2021 · Glossary Arrangement

    Fix a height and let a prefix DP minimize the required width. Height feasibility is monotone, so the outer optimization becomes a binary search.

    Read the lesson · Binary search on the answer
  • 2024 · Limited Library

    A contrast worth learning: fitting a book on one shelf permits it on every taller shelf. Reduce capacity on shorter shelves first, then greedily check a proposed number. The jury uses binary search and greedy matching.

    Read the lesson · Range minimum with the original index · Bottom-up segment tree
  • 2024 · Dutch Democracy

    Requiring every selected party to be necessary collapses to removing the smallest. Count exact prefix sums in the narrow window that crosses a strict majority when this party enters.

    Read the lesson · Count minimal winning coalitions
  • 2025 · Bisecting Bargain

    A bitset tests one proposed collection of notes. Enumerating all collections remains too slow. Exchange equivalent denominations first to reduce the outer search.

    Read the lesson · Find a payout that cannot be split · Bitset subset sum
  • 2025 · Group Photo

    A quadratic grid over left/right placements contains only linearly many rewarding events. Retain their order constraints and search for a chain, or maintain the same DP with a range structure.

    Read the lesson · Strict longest increasing subsequence

  • 2022 · Crashing Competition Computer

    Progress and crash risk define a first-step expectation. A restart term must retain the expected work that will be repeated.

    Read the lesson · First-step expectation and probability
  • 2023 · Jungle Job

    Count connected subsets once by their highest vertex and by size. Multiplying child options performs the size split automatically.

    Read the lesson · Rooted tree DP and rerooting · 0/1 knapsack
  • 2024 · Museum Visit

    Delete intervals containing another required interval, then the compatible preceding choices form a sliding range. A minimum-cost prefix state can query that range.

  • 2025 · Garbage In, Garbage Out

    A dictionary hypothesis is a prefix segmentation question. Testing allowable token lengths turns a classification story into Boolean word-break DP.
