Restrict the previous choices · Lesson 151 of 235

A forbidden gap can turn one predecessor query into two

Choose a long subsequence of heights where consecutive values must differ by at least 4. Which old heights can lead into h?

1. Work one example

Exclude the forbidden gap around h. The remaining heights form a low interval and a high interval. Query the best chain in both, extend the better one by one, and preserve its last index if you need the chain.

2. What do we need to remember?

A forbidden middle band leaves two allowed ranges. Query each range and compare their answers.

3. How do we get the next answer?

Process a sequence in input order. If a prior height h is legal when |h−x|≥d, its height belongs to a prefix ending at x−d or a suffix starting at x+d. Compress heights and store the best length together with its last index.

D(i)=1+max⁡j<ihj≤hi−d ∨ hj≥hi+dD(j)D(i)=1+\max_{\substack{j<i\\h_j\le h_i-d\ \lor\ h_j\ge h_i+d}}D(j)D(i)=1+j<ihj​≤hi​−d ∨ hj​≥hi​+d​max​D(j)

D[i]=1+max over the two ranges of D[j] with j<i. Query before inserting i. Insert at rank hᵢ by taking the better of the previous leaf and (D[i],i).

Check the boundary. When d=0 the ranges overlap at x. Max is idempotent, but adding counts of optima would count overlap twice. Merge disjoint ranges or handle this case as one full-range query.

Cost O(n log n) including compression and parent reconstruction, O(n) space. Prefix and suffix Fenwick maxima also work if updates only improve a leaf.

Try one change

You allow positions 1 through 9 except 4 through 6. Which two queries cover exactly the legal choices?

See the reasoning

Query 1 through 3 and 7 through 9, then combine their results. One query across the whole interval could choose a forbidden position.

From idea to code

  • Bottom-up segment tree
    The original combine is addition over a half-open interval. For a min, max, witness or matrix payload, update the stored type, combine and empty identity together. Preserve combination order for matrices.
  • Coordinate compression
    Compression preserves order and equality. Convert open and closed query bounds with lower_bound and upper_bound. Original distances must still use the uncompressed coordinates.
