A narrow frontier remembers occupied cells

How many ways tile a 2 by 3 board with dominoes? What must survive after you finish one column?

Work through the choice

A horizontal domino placed here occupies a square in the next column. Record those protrusions as bits. Fully filling a column produces a new mask, and the last column must leave the empty mask.

The rule you can reuse

Sweep a long thin board. Once a column is fully covered, the future sees only which cells in the next column already belong to horizontal dominoes. Generate the next mask by filling the first empty cell.

Fi+1(b)=∑aFi(a) T(a,b)F_{i+1}(b)=\sum_a F_i(a)\,T(a,b)Fi+1​(b)=a∑​Fi​(a)T(a,b)

For incoming mask m, either place a vertical domino on two adjacent free rows, or cover one row horizontally and set that bit in outgoing mask. Add ways[m] to every resulting mask.

Check the boundary. Account for blocked cells on both sides of a crossing. Two placements producing the same next mask must retain their multiplicity in counting variants.

Cost Let E be the number of valid mask transitions, width L and height h. O(LE) after preprocessing, O(2ʰ) DP cells, plus stored transitions.
