Count paths without visiting every cell · Lesson 184 of 235

Replace a huge symmetric board by four probability or count states

A rook stands on an empty rectangular chessboard. Each move goes to a different square in the same row or column. Given a target and k, count the ways to finish on that target after exactly k moves. On a 3 × 4 board, try returning to the starting square in two moves.

1. Work one example

Classify a square by whether its row and column match the target. From any two squares in one class, the numbers of moves into each of the four classes agree. Keep totals for these four classes and weight each transition by its number of destinations.

2. What do we need to remember?

Cells with the same row and column relationship to the target have identical future move counts. Combine them into classes.

3. How do we get the next answer?

For a square off both target lines, changing only its column can hit the target column once. Changing only its row can hit the target row once. Count the other moves within the appropriate class.

Check the boundary. Obstacles or square-specific rewards can break symmetry. With a dimension of size one, omit impossible classes or derive counts from actual coordinates.

Cost O(k) with four states, or O(log k) with a constant-size matrix power.

Try one change

Relative to a target, a rook may share its row, column, both, or neither. Why must you still count legal destinations in each class?

See the reasoning

An individual class can represent many cells. The number of moves to each destination class supplies its transition multiplicity.

From idea to code

  • Matrix multiplication and power
    The sheet multiplies with ordinary modular addition and multiplication. For minimum-cost transitions replace those by min and plus, use an infinite unreachable value, and skip unreachable additions.
  • Two-state DP with rolling layers
    Track the best score when the preceding house was skipped or taken. Each step reads the old pair. Adapt it by naming the histories a future move can distinguish.
