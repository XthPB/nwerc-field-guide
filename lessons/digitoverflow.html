Digit products can return from above the limit

Given an upper bound, count positive integers whose decimal digits have product at most 5. An internal zero makes the whole product zero. When building a candidate beginning with 9, later digits can still affect whether it qualifies.

1. Work one example

Prefix 9 exceeds the limit, but appending zero gives 90 with product zero. Keep one overflow state for positive products above the threshold. A real zero sends every started state, even overflow, to zero.

2. What do we need to remember?

A later zero can reset an oversized product to zero. Keep that possibility alive until the number is finished.

Store feasible positive products in a sparse map or enumerate products of powers of 2, 3, 5 and 7. Handle a first nonzero digit by setting the product to that digit.

Check the boundary. When K is large, check p > K/d before multiplying by positive d. Handle d=0 first.

Cost O(10·digits·S) transitions for S retained product states plus start, zero and overflow.
