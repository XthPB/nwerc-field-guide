Walk across a grid · Lesson 25 of 235

Count paths with a limited number of turns

A walker crosses a 3 by 3 board from corner to corner, going right or down. How many paths use at most one turn?

1. Work one example

Exactly two such paths exist, RRDD and DDRR. At each cell save the direction of the last step, because stepping down adds a turn only when the last step was right. Add a small counter for how many turns have been spent.

2. What do we need to remember?

Two paths reaching one cell can afford different continuations. Remember their last direction and how many turns they spent.

Seed a legal first move with zero turns. At the destination sum all permitted turn counts and final directions. Blocked cells have no outgoing paths.

Check the boundary. Handle a start equal to the destination separately. A fictitious direction at the start must not charge the first move.

Cost O(HWK) time and O(WK) memory with rolling rows.
