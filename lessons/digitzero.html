Make leading zeros and the actual number zero behave correctly

How many integers from 0 through 20 contain a zero in their ordinary decimal representation?

Work through the choice

Write 7 as 07 only for alignment with the bound. That first zero has no numerical digit position. A started flag tells you when a zero is a real digit. Handle the all-padding path as the number zero at the end.

The rule you can reuse

s′=s∨(d>0),z′=z∨(s∧d=0)s'=s\lor(d>0),\qquad z'=z\lor(s\land d=0)s′=s∨(d>0),z′=z∨(s∧d=0)

Keep tight, started and hasSeenZero. At the end accept hasSeenZero or the never-started path when the canonical representation of zero should satisfy the predicate.

Check the boundary. For fixed-width identifiers, every padded zero may be a real symbol. Choose the representation from the statement before selecting the initial state.

Cost O(10·digits) with a constant number of flags.
