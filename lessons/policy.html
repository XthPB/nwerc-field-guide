‹ Probability, expectations and games

Choose before or after observing a random outcome

Must you guess a fair coin before the toss, or may you pick an answer after seeing it?

Work through the choice

Before seeing the toss you must pick one action for every possible outcome. After observing it you may choose a separate action in each branch. This decides whether the maximum sits outside or inside the weighted sum.

The rule you can reuse

For a decision followed by chance, first compute the expectation for each available action, then optimize. When the outcome is observed before choosing, the optimization belongs inside the expectation.

V[s]=maxₐ Σₜ p(t|s,a)(reward(s,a,t)+V[t]). In a finite horizon index the remaining decisions to give a DAG.

Check the boundary. Actions based on hidden information are invalid. Shared unknown parameters induce dependence even when observations are conditionally independent. Carry a sufficient posterior statistic if the rules allow adaptation.

Cost O(horizon × states × actions × outcomes) for direct finite-horizon tabulation. Infinite-horizon processes require termination or another convergence argument.
