Optimize a ratio by subtracting a trial price

Select exactly two items to maximize total benefit divided by total time. Their (benefit,time) pairs are (6,2), (4,1), (7,4). How do you compare complete selections without storing all possible totals?

1. Work one example

Test a candidate ratio q. An admissible choice reaches it exactly when its total benefit minus q times its total time is nonnegative. These transformed item scores add, so an ordinary selection DP can optimize the inner objective.

2. What do we need to remember?

Test a proposed ratio by converting every choice to an additive gain. A feasible nonnegative total answers the test.

Use any DP needed to select a legal group under the original structural constraints. For a rational test q=p/d, compare integer sums of d·benefit−p·time when the ranges allow.

Check the boundary. All candidate denominators must be positive. Fix a precision justified by the output requirements, or derive an exact rational selection method when exact comparison is required.

Try one change

You want value divided by positive weight at least 2. A candidate has value 11 and weight 5. How does the transformed test read?

See the reasoning

11−2×5=1, so it passes. Across choices, optimize the transformed additive score at a fixed trial ratio.

From idea to code

  • Binary search on the answer
    Supply a feasibility predicate and prove it changes truth value in only one direction. Check the feasible upper bound and the loop's inclusive endpoints.
  • 0/1 knapsack
    Use the descending capacity loop for one-use items. The initial zeros in this implementation mean “at most this capacity.” For exact weight, mark initially unreachable cells before the first update.
