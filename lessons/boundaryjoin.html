‹ Meet in the middle

Meet on a grid, a hash, or a boundary state

Count right/down paths with a target XOR without enumerating every full path.

Work through the choice

Every path crosses one middle diagonal cell. Enumerate half-paths from each corner and combine only those meeting at the same cell. If you included that cell in both XORs, apply its value once more so it counts once.

The rule you can reuse

The two halves can be stretches of a path. Choose a separator hit exactly once. Enumerate partial paths to each meeting cell, then join only compatible summaries at that cell. Differences, XOR and residues give simple complements.

Xright=K⊕Xleft⊕vmeetingX_{\rm right}=K\mathbin{\oplus}X_{\rm left}\mathbin{\oplus}v_{\rm meeting}Xright​=K⊕Xleft​⊕vmeeting​

If both partial XORs include meeting value v, full XOR = left XOR right XOR v. For target K, look up right = K XOR left XOR v.

Check the boundary. A single common dictionary across all meeting cells splices disconnected walks. A probabilistic hash match requires a collision policy for exact answers.

Cost With D total monotone moves, about O(2^⌈D/2⌉) partial paths per side as an upper bound, plus join costs. A separator can have uneven side workloads.
