Cut the search in half · Lesson 71 of 235

Profit under capacity, several resources, and exact size

Each item has a weight and a profit, and can be taken at most once. You split the collection into two halves to maximize profit under a weight limit. One left-hand choice leaves 3 units free. Find which right-hand choice gives the most profit that still fits.

1. Work one example

Sort the right choices by weight and record the best profit anywhere up to each index. The last affordable choice may have poor profit. Its running best includes all the lighter alternatives.

2. What do we need to remember?

Store how much of the second resource each half used. Join only pairs that meet both the value condition and the combined budget.

3. How do we get the next answer?

A right summary may have weight, profit, cardinality and a mask. Sort by weight and build prefix-best profit. For a left choice, search the last right weight ≤ remaining capacity. Group by cardinality when size is fixed.

max⁡(w,p)∈L(p+max⁡(w′,p′)∈R: w′≤W−wp′)\max_{(w,p)\in L}\left(p+\max_{(w',p')\in R:\,w'\le W-w}p'\right)(w,p)∈Lmax​(p+(w′,p′)∈R:w′≤W−wmax​p′)

max over L of (profitL + prefixBestR[upper_bound(W−weightL)−1]). For exactly k items join bucket c only with bucket k−c.

Check the boundary. Sorting by weight alone and taking the last feasible record can lose a higher profit at lower weight. Two independent budgets require a dominance query on the other coordinate as well.

Cost O(2ʰ log 2ʰ) for one upper bound. An offline sweep plus a compressed Fenwick prefix maximum handles two monotone bounds in O(M log M) for M half-states and queries.

Try one change

Your left half used six units of a ten-unit capacity. Which right half wins among weights 3 and 5 worth 8 and 10?

See the reasoning

Weight 3 wins because only four units remain. A search sorted by weight must return the best profit in its eligible prefix.

From idea to code

  • Meet in the middle for profit
    Use the prefix-best pass on the weight-sorted right list. When reconstructing a choice, update its stored witness whenever its prefix profit improves. This snippet assumes nonnegative weights.
