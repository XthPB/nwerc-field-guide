Keep a frontier when one score per position fails

Your backpack holds 6. You can carry weight 2 for profit 5 or weight 4 for profit 4. Which options deserve space in the table?

Work through the choice

Anything that fits after the heavier choice also fits after the lighter one, which already earned more. Discard the heavier choice. A heavier choice earning 9 must stay because another continuation could favour it.

The rule you can reuse

At a fixed step compare resource used and profit already earned. Discard (w₂,v₂) if another has w₁≤w₂ and v₁≥v₂ and every later choice remains feasible after replacing the former by the latter.

w1≤w2, v1≥v2⟹(w1,v1) dominates (w2,v2)w_1\le w_2,\ v_1\ge v_2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(w_1,v_1)\text{ dominates }(w_2,v_2)w1​≤w2​, v1​≥v2​⟹(w1​,v1​) dominates (w2​,v2​)

Sort by increasing weight, retain strictly increasing record profits after coalescing equal weights.

Check the boundary. For exact final weight, a lighter prefix can miss the target. Counting and enumeration also lose the multiplicities of discarded histories.

Cost Merging two sorted frontiers for one optional item costs O(F) for frontier size F. F can still reach 2ⁿ.
