‹ Dynamic programming on trees

Send each child the contribution of all its siblings

For every vertex of a tree, count the connected sets of vertices that contain it. Can you answer for every possible root in linear time, even modulo 6?

Work through the choice

Cut an edge joining a parent and a child. A connected set containing the parent may skip the child's side, or include a connected set anchored at that child. Multiply these one-plus-message factors. To send a message back towards one child, leave out just its own factor.

One vertex sends three different messages

1 / 4

Collect the three incoming factors

Suppose adjacent branches independently offer two, three and four choices including the option to skip. All factors together give 24, or 0 modulo 6.

Send to the first neighbour

Exclude its factor 2. The other sides offer 3 × 4 = 12 choices, or 0 modulo 6.

Send to the second neighbour

Exclude its factor 3. The other sides offer 2 × 4 = 8 choices, or 2 modulo 6. The full product was zero, so it could not tell us this answer.

Send to the third neighbour

Exclude its factor 4. The other sides offer 2 × 3 = 6 choices, or 0 modulo 6. Prefix and suffix products compute each outgoing message without division.

The rule you can reuse

mu→v=∏x∈N(u)∖{v}(1+mx→u)m_{u\to v}=\prod_{x\in N(u)\setminus\{v\}}\left(1+m_{x\to u}\right)mu→v​=x∈N(u)∖{v}∏​(1+mx→u​)

Do one bottom-up pass for child messages. At u, form prefix and suffix products in neighbour order. Their product across the gap gives every leave-one-out value in O(1) after O(degree) preprocessing. Then send those values top-down.

Check the boundary. A factor may be zero or share a divisor with the modulus, so modular division is unsafe. A leaf sends one and an isolated vertex has one connected set, reduced modulo M.

Cost O(n) time and O(n) total storage, since degrees add up to twice the edge count.

From idea to code

  • Connected sets by rerooting with prefix and suffix products
    Two directed messages per tree edge compute every vertex answer. Prefix and suffix products exclude a child without division. Composite moduli and zero factors are supported.
  • Rooted tree DP and rerooting
    Use reverse traversal for bottom-up child messages. Use forward traversal for the parent contribution. Derive a new message when the objective differs from distance or subtree size.
See the idea in contest problems
  • AtCoder DP · Subtree

    Count connected choices on each side of a directed edge. Use leave-one-child-out products for the reverse pass, which is valid for composite moduli.
