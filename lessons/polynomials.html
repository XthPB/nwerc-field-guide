Convolve independent count distributions

Two labeled weights of 2 and one weight of 3 can be chosen independently. How many selections reach 5?

Work through the choice

Represent each item by one term for skipping and one for taking. Multiplying picks one of these terms per item. Exponents add to the total weight and coefficients count how often that total appears.

The rule you can reuse

When independent groups add sizes, encode ways of taking j items as coefficients. Multiplying polynomials performs all splits simultaneously. Truncate degrees greater than K when degrees are nonnegative and larger ones cannot contribute back.

If A and B are count polynomials, [zᵏ](AB)=Σᵢ A[i]B[k−i]. A labeled one-use item has factor (1+zʷ).

Check the boundary. FFT/NTT is for ordinary convolution, not arbitrary max-plus knapsack. An NTT needs an appropriate root of unity and transform length. Modular coefficient zero does not certify exact infeasibility.

Cost Naive O(ab) for lengths a,b. NTT O(L log L) for supported padded L≥a+b−1. Many factors often benefit from a balanced product tree.

From idea to code

  • Number-theoretic transform
    Use this for ordinary polynomial convolution with the supported modulus and transform length. Choose an appropriate root and power-of-two length and bring modular exponentiation from the linked arithmetic section.
  • 0/1 knapsack
    Use the descending capacity loop for one-use items. The initial zeros in this implementation mean “at most this capacity.” For exact weight, mark initially unreachable cells before the first update.
