Recover the most likely hidden state sequence

A sensor observes signals but hides whether a machine is healthy or faulty at each time. Which complete hidden history best explains the observations?

Work through the choice

Two histories ending in the same hidden state have exactly the same possible future transitions. Keep only the more probable history for that final state. Multiplying by the next transition and emission probabilities gives candidate extensions.

The rule you can reuse

Vt(j)=Pr⁡(ot∣j)max⁡i{Vt−1(i)Pr⁡(j∣i)}V_t(j)=\Pr(o_t\mid j)\max_i\{V_{t-1}(i)\Pr(j\mid i)\}Vt​(j)=Pr(ot​∣j)imax​{Vt−1​(i)Pr(j∣i)}

Initialize using the prior distribution and the first observation. Store the maximizing predecessor for reconstruction. Logs turn products into sums and avoid underflow on long sequences.

Check the boundary. The most probable state separately at every time can form a different sequence from the most probable complete path. Impossible events have logarithm negative infinity.

Cost O(TS²) for dense transitions, or O(T·edges) for a sparse model.
