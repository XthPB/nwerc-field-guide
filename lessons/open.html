‹ Sequences, scheduling and sweeps

Sort events and count open groups

Split sorted heights into groups and charge each group its tallest height minus its shortest. How can you price unfinished groups?

Work through the choice

Sweep between consecutive heights. Every group already started and still open spans the entire gap, so it incurs that gap once. Knowing how many groups are open is enough to price the step.

The rule you can reuse

When group cost is maximum minus minimum, sweep coordinates. Every unfinished group pays the distance to the next coordinate once. Group identities may be interchangeable, while multiplicities of placement choices still matter.

Check the boundary. Start at zero open groups and finish at zero. Include the j factors for the join and close cases when grouping labeled items. For equal values use a fixed item order.
