‹ Digits, bounds and carries

Sum digits, sum values, or compute moments without a sum axis

You know there are two prefixes, 1 and 3, with total value 4. What happens to their combined value if both append digit 5?

Work through the choice

Appending 5 multiplies each prefix by 10 and adds 5 once per prefix. The count and sum carry enough information to compute the new sum. Higher moments need corresponding extra aggregates.

The rule you can reuse

When only an aggregate over all accepted numbers is requested, carry a count and an accumulated statistic in each state. A whole distribution is needed only when some future decision inspects the individual statistic.

(C,S)⟼(C,bS+dC)(C,S)\longmapsto(C,bS+dC)(C,S)⟼(C,bS+dC)

Appending d in base b maps (c,S) to (c,bS+dc). For sum of digit sums use (c,S+dc). Aggregate alternative branches componentwise.

Check the boundary. For squared numeric values also store Q: Q′=b²Q+2bdS+d²c. For modular output apply the modulus consistently to every component.

Cost Constant extra arithmetic per transition for a fixed number of moments. Caps or predicates on exact totals generally reintroduce a state dimension.
