Walk across a grid · Lesson 23 of 235

Separate a monotone walk from a general shortest path

A board has two rows and three columns. You start in the upper-left corner and want to reach the lower-right corner. You may step one cell right or down, and the top middle cell is blocked. Count the different routes that avoid it.

1. Work one example

Every route reaches a cell from above or from the left. Add these disjoint possibilities in row order. A blocked cell contributes zero. On this board the only surviving route goes down first.

2. What do we need to remember?

The row and column specify where you are. Enumerate the moves that could have entered this cell.

3. How do we get the next answer?

A right/down lattice has rank row+column. Store count, best reward or minimum cost per cell and combine the two predecessors. Walls force unreachable values.

Dr,c=wr,c+min⁡(Dr−1,c,Dr,c−1)D_{r,c}=w_{r,c}+\min(D_{r-1,c},D_{r,c-1})Dr,c​=wr,c​+min(Dr−1,c​,Dr,c−1​)

d[r,c]=w[r,c]+min(d[r−1,c],d[r,c−1]) for minimum additive path; for counts add predecessor counts with no cell reward.

Check the boundary. If left/up moves are allowed, row-major order no longer respects all edges. Use BFS or Dijkstra for appropriate costs. Negative cycles are a separate possibility.

Cost O(HW) time and O(W) rolling space, unless a witness requires parent data.

Try one change

At the upper-left corner there is one empty path. With only down and right moves, how many reach the opposite corner of a 2 by 2 board?

See the reasoning

Two. Each of its two predecessors has one path and the last move distinguishes the two sets.

  • CF 1353F · Decreasing Heights 2200

    Walk down or right through a grid. You may pay one per unit to lower a cell value. Find the least cost that makes the values along some corner-to-corner path increase by exactly one per step.

    Along an increasing right/down route, final height minus row minus column is constant. Try each input cell's adjusted height as that constant, forbid cells too low, and use minimum-cost monotone paths for each candidate.
