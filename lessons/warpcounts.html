Index a lattice DP by move counts when coordinates are huge

A robot has three fixed jump vectors and must take exactly n jumps without landing on a forbidden point. Coordinates can reach billions. What else can identify a landing?

Work through the choice

After t jumps, let i and j count how often the first two vectors were used. The third was used exactly t−i−j times. These counts reconstruct the coordinate and give just three ways to reach the state.

The rule you can reuse

p=iA+jB+(t−i−j)Cp=iA+jB+(t-i-j)Cp=iA+jB+(t−i−j)C

Store forbidden coordinate pairs in a set. Build the next layer by trying the three moves from every live count pair and test its computed destination.

Check the boundary. Different count triples can yield the same coordinate when vectors are dependent. They encode disjoint move sequences. Sum all final states when the task counts sequences.

Cost O(n³) transitions and O(n²) rolling storage with expected O(1) hash lookups.
