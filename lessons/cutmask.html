Choose a set and its endpoint · Lesson 82 of 235

Charge a pair whenever a growing cut separates it

You’re given symbols and a weight for each related pair. Place the symbols in distinct positions on a line. A pair pays its weight times the number of positions separating it. Find an ordering that minimizes the total payment.

1. Work one example

A pair distance equals the number of gaps between its two positions. When you grow the order from the left, charge a pair once at every cut that separates its endpoints. The chosen set determines the price of that cut.

2. What do we need to remember?

A placed subset determines which weighted edges cross the current cut. Charge that cut as you extend the ordering.

3. How do we get the next answer?

If the score sums pair distance in a permutation, grow the prefix set. A pair separated by d positions crosses exactly d prefix cuts. The cost of a cut depends only on the set to its left, which eliminates the need for a last endpoint.

cut[M]=Σ_{u∈M,v∉M} w[u,v], with unordered pair weights. D[M]=min over v∈M D[M without v]+cut[M] and cut[full]=0.

Check the boundary. This decomposition assumes linear distance. A squared distance cannot be charged by a constant per crossed cut without extra information.

Cost O(b2ᵇ) time including cut preprocessing by incrementally adding one vertex, O(2ᵇ) extra space.
