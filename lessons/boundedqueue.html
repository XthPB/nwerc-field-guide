‹ Knapsack and subset sum

Remove a large multiplicity with a residue-class deque

You can buy at most two copies of an item weighing 2. How do you update every capacity without trying all copy counts?

Work through the choice

At capacity 6 the old capacity can be 6, 4 or 2. All have the same remainder modulo 2. Along that list the allowed old indices form a sliding window. Subtract their predictable share of the profit and maintain the best remaining value.

The rule you can reuse

With c identical items of weight w>0 and value v, indices in different residue classes never interact. Express the predecessor index as r+jw. After subtracting the linear part, all legal j lie in a sliding window.

D′(r+kw)=kv+max⁡max⁡(0,k−c)≤j≤k(D(r+jw)−jv)D'(r+kw)=kv+\max_{\max(0,k-c)\le j\le k}\bigl(D(r+jw)-jv\bigr)D′(r+kw)=kv+max(0,k−c)≤j≤kmax​(D(r+jw)−jv)

new[r+kw] = kv + max over max(0,k−c)≤j≤k of (old[r+jw]−jv).

Check the boundary. Build candidates from the old layer. Exclude unreachable −∞ states and expire indices by position, including when scores tie.

Cost O(W) per type across all residues. Replace max with a sliding sum for indistinguishable bounded counting.
