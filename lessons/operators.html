Maintain a composition when positions change

House rewards change after updates. How can you maintain the best nonadjacent selection of the whole street?

Work through the choice

One house transforms a pair of incoming best scores into a pair of outgoing ones. Store the whole transformation for each segment. Compose left and right in street order. Only ancestors of the changed house require work.

The rule you can reuse

If each position applies a small linear, min-plus, or max-plus transfer, an interval summary is the product of its position operators. Associativity permits a segment tree and local edits. For branching histories retain versions.

MAB=MBMAfor column vectors, reading A then BM_{AB}=M_BM_A\qquad\text{for column vectors, reading }A\text{ then }BMAB​=MB​MA​for column vectors, reading A then B

For column vectors and left block A followed by right block B, merged operator M=M_B M_A. Padding leaves use identity.

Check the boundary. Noncommutative order matters. Concatenating blocks may require boundary states, not a single best value per block. With large state dimension a matrix at every node can be unaffordable.

Cost O(s³ log n) per update or range product for dense s-state matrices, often constant s. O(ns²) storage.

From idea to code

  • Bottom-up segment tree
    The original combine is addition over a half-open interval. For a min, max, witness or matrix payload, update the stored type, combine and empty identity together. Preserve combination order for matrices.
  • Matrix multiplication and power
    The sheet multiplies with ordinary modular addition and multiplication. For minimum-cost transitions replace those by min and plus, use an infinite unreachable value, and skip unreachable additions.
