Shrink the knapsack axis · Lesson 64 of 235

Repeated weights and the square-root bound

You have five identical items weighing 3 each. You may take any quantity from zero to five. You want to determine which total weights fit a target without processing five separate copies. A quantity of six must stay impossible.

1. Work one example

Replace two copies by one double-weight copy only when enough single copies remain to realize odd choices. Repeating with a suitable rule reduces repeated work. This argument preserves existence and needs a different treatment for counting ways.

2. What do we need to remember?

Many equal items repeat the same shift. Compress them while preserving which multiplicities can actually be made.

3. How do we get the next answer?

If the sum S is moderate but there are many repeated positive integer weights, inspect how many different shifts a bitset really needs. For feasibility you may turn two copies of w into one of 2w provided enough w remains to form all intermediate totals.

cw←cw−2,c2w←c2w+1(cw≥3)c_w\gets c_w-2,\quad c_{2w}\gets c_{2w}+1\qquad(c_w\ge3)cw​←cw​−2,c2w​←c2w​+1(cw​≥3)

While count[w]≥3, replace two copies of w with one copy of 2w. Process weights in increasing order. Each remaining weight then occurs at most twice.

Check the boundary. At least one old copy must remain for this carry. This preserves achievable totals only, not labeled counts, exact cardinality, or arbitrary unequal profits.

Cost At most O(√S) distinct positive weights can remain because 1+…+d≤S, and each occurs ≤2 times. After grouping, O(√S·⌈(S+1)/word⌉) bitset word operations plus scanning counts.

Try one change

Five identical weight-3 objects are available. Can bundles of 1, 2 and 2 objects represent every allowed count?

See the reasoning

Yes, from zero to five. This preserves reachability and the value of identical copies. Different bundle choices can encode the same count, so plain bundle counting can overcount.

    Use the descending capacity loop for one-use items. The initial zeros in this implementation mean “at most this capacity.” For exact weight, mark initially unreachable cells before the first update.
