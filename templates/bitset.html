Bitset subset sum

Set only bit zero at the start. A shifted copy represents taking one item. OR retains the outcomes of skipping it. Choose MAXS large enough for the actual largest queried sum.

From the ICPC Code Templates cheat sheet.

// subset sum: reach[s] = 1 if a subset sums to s, O(n*MAXS/64)
bitset<100001> reach;
reach[0] = 1;
for (int x : items) reach |= reach << x;

These are contest snippets. Bring the declarations they use into your file, including standard headers, the ll alias or the modulus where applicable.
