Knapsack by profit

Choose the total profit as the array index. Store the minimum weight for each exact profit and scan for the largest affordable one. These two lines are the original sheet's design note.

From the ICPC Code Templates cheat sheet.

// weight/time ~1e9 but small answer (<= ~5000): swap the axes, dp[j] =

These are contest snippets. Bring the declarations they use into your file, including standard headers, the ll alias or the modulus where applicable.

Lessons using this template

  • Index by moves when the answer is smaller than the search space
  • Remove a shared weight before indexing the capacity
