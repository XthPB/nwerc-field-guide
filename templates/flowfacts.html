FLOW FACTS (use the Dinic code above)
  Bipartite matching: s->left, left->right, right->t, all cap 1.
    Dinic is O(E sqrt V) here. Pairs: left->right edges, flow 1.
  Min cut: after flow(), v is on the source side iff
    level[v] != -1. Cut = edges from source side to sink side.
  Vertex capacity c: split v into v_in -> v_out (cap c).
  Undirected edge {u, v}: add_edge(u, v, c) and add_edge(v, u, c).
  Bipartite: max matching = min vertex cover,
    max independent set = V - max matching.
  DAG, fewest vertex-disjoint paths covering all nodes:
    V - matching (edge u->v gives left u -> right v).
  Choose projects: profit p: s->proj cap p; cost c: proj->t cap c;
    "A needs B": A->B cap INF. Best = sum of profits - maxflow.
  Hall: all of left matchable iff |N(S)| >= |S| for every S.
