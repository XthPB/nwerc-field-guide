What the DP guide covers in practice

Search all topics, ratings and tag combinations · New advanced DP workshops

We drew 1,500 Codeforces problems carrying the official DP tag, from 800 through 3000. The question for each was whether a lesson teaches the move that makes its solution work.

Each problem has a written observation. The audit gives 299 solution-level verdicts and 1,201 shorter checks of a particular idea.

What the individual checks showed

Ten problems at each of the 23 ratings received a separate solution-level review. A lesson earned “Taught” when it explained the main reduction and transition for a viable route. “Partial” means there is an identifiable extra step, such as proving that the candidates can be put in order or computing their range at contest speed.

    How the sample and checks were made

    The snapshot contains 2,315 rated DP-tagged problem IDs. We grouped entries with the same contest start time and normalized title, including common easy/hard suffixes. That left 2,233 groups. Different events retain separate entries. A second check found six pairs with the same contest and problem letter but different version names or objectives. The 1,500 IDs therefore span 1,494 contest-and-letter groups. The seeded draw chooses a representative within each group, then samples proportionally within each 100-point rating band. There are 46 problems at exactly 3000. No sampled task was exchanged for one with a more accessible solution. Codeforces API · Frozen sampling frame · Reproduce the selection

    The seed is nwerc-dp-random-audit-2026-11-23-v1. The separate ten-per-rating panel orders sampled IDs by SHA-256 of independent-panel-v1|ID in each rating and takes ten. The remaining detailed reviews include additional low-rated examples, every sampled geometry and meet-in-the-middle task, and selected hybrids. These extra reviews are deliberately targeted.

    Depth of inspection. All 1,500 sources went through the same retrieval test against 228 pre-audit lessons. 299 problems also have an individual solution-level verdict. Another 1201 have a shorter, separately recorded technique check. Among these, 964 have a located component, 144 have an alternative route, and 93 remain inconclusive. 0 have retrieval evidence alone. A located component confirms a specific step and leaves the remainder of the solution open.

    1439 rows link to a solution write-up or implementation. The other 61 use a problem statement. Editorials came from Codeforces authors and public collections. The CSV also includes named sections from independent technique indexes where available. The archive entry for CF 1557C described its neighbor CF 1557D; its published note uses the correct alternate editorial.     Calibration. Lexical and multilingual semantic searches used up to three source passages, ranked all 228 lessons, then reranked seven candidates. The problem IDs, official tags and existing problem-to-lesson catalog were excluded from the search features. Of the 164 panel cases with a lesson identified on review, the first three suggestions contained a checked lesson in 26. A few exact words or a nearby embedding are weak evidence of full transfer. The individual notes take precedence. Multilingual E5 · Reranker
