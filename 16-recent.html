Newer Codeforces ideas

If the tag is still DP, what completely different representation makes the new problem tractable?

  • Undo an invertible decision chainA sequence of reflections has to end at zero and you are missing its first centre. How can you recover it?
  • Quotient rounding can leave only two states per depthRepeatedly split an integer pile into two halves rounded down and up. Can a descendant of 19 have size 5?
  • A cap on the maximum becomes bounded denomination stockYou minimize a maximum by guessing it. After fixing the guess, the allowed group sizes have limited stock. Which totals can these sizes make?
  • Summarize a deterministic subtree traversal that resets its own memoryA deterministic walker explores child branches in a fixed cyclic order. How many steps pass before it returns through its parent edge?
  • Compress edge-pair transitions by the previous labelCount directed paths whose vertex labels form a Fibonacci-like sequence. What does the last edge tell you about a valid preceding edge?
  • One family of intervals contributes a ramp at every covered indexFor each character position, count substrings through it that satisfy a subsequence rule. How can whole families of substring ends contribute together?
  • A predecessor must become eligible before a range maximum can see itChoose weighted positions on a line whose pairwise spacing is large enough for both endpoints. When does an old position become usable?
  • Turn “all pairs are compatible” into a chain through each objectChoose as many curves as possible that can be consistently stacked without crossing. How can a pairwise condition become a sequence DP?
  • Exact nearest-point distances turn into clauses on adjacent surviving sitesA clue says the nearest treasure is exactly two sites away. How do you turn an exact distance into conditions on individual sites?
  • A stochastic process can reduce to choosing a deterministic orderA tree vertex changes colour when a randomly checked neighbour already has the desired colour. Which vertex should you attempt next to minimize the expected time?
  • Only four witnesses determine a geometric scorePick points to retain while balancing bounding-box perimeter against the price of retaining each point. How many points actually determine the perimeter?
  • Expand a square and factor the coefficient of a pairSum a squared rectangle score over many rectangles of a grid. How can you avoid visiting every pair of grid cells for every rectangle?
  • Count strings that contain every allowed subsequenceCount words that contain every permitted target subsequence when each stage allows several possible letters.
  • Build a long DP row by reusing slicesOne update copies two long pieces of yesterday's DP array in a new order. How can you process many days without physically copying every cell?
  • Classify vertices by the complete paths that contain themIn a directed acyclic grid, which cells occur on exactly the same family of complete start-to-finish paths?
  • Represent a convex tree DP by its sorted marginal costsEach child of a tree has a cost for selecting 0, 1 or 2 units. How can you merge their costs when each extra unit costs at least as much as the previous one?
  • Move a tree-cut DP into the XOR span of allowed totalsCut a labeled tree so every component's XOR belongs to a small allowed set. Can you avoid a table over every raw label?

Test your recognition
In a cut-tree problem, weights have 30 bits but every completed component must equal one of six listed XORs. What bounds your state count?

Completed-component XORs belong to the binary span of the six allowed values, of dimension at most six. Track closed XOR in that basis; the open XOR follows from the fixed subtree XOR.

Two cells have the same number of complete paths passing through them. May they share a universal co-occurrence class?

Equality of counts is insufficient. In a diamond the two branch cells each see one path, but the paths differ. Test mutual dominance and postdominance or an equivalent exact path-set criterion.
