Greedy, sorting and search · 11 of 15

Turn an optimization target into a decision

Machines finish one item every 2 and 3 minutes. Find the earliest time with five finished items. Trying all assignments would solve far more than required.

Work through the example

  1. At time 5 the outputs are 2 and 1. Five items are impossible.
  2. At time 6 the outputs are 3 and 2. Five items are possible.
  3. Every later time keeps all finished items. Binary search for the first true time, with a known false lower bound and true upper bound.

Why it works

The predicate is monotone because waiting can only add items. A feasibility check can itself be greedy, a flow, a DP, or a two-pointer pass. Prove the checker and monotonicity separately.

When the conditions change

O(n log U) for n machines and upper bound U. Saturate the sum at the target or use a wide type. For recipe production sum ingredient deficits; ordinary binary search also works when the resource that can fill any deficit is shared.

Try one change

Can binary search find a time at which exactly five items finish in the same minute?

Show the reasoning

The equality predicate need not stay true at later times. A first-threshold predicate has the required ordering.
