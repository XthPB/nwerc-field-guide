Average fixed colorings over symmetries

How many two-color necklaces of length four are different up to rotation? The 16 labeled strings do not all have four distinct rotations.

Work through the example

  1. The identity fixes 16 strings. A rotation by one or three fixes just the two constant strings.
  2. A rotation by two fixes strings of the form ABAB, yielding 4. The average (16+2+4+2)/4 is 6.
  3. For length n, rotating by k has gcd(n,k) position cycles, each with one independent color choice.

Why it works

Burnside’s lemma counts pairs (coloring, symmetry fixing it) two ways. Shorter orbits have more symmetries fixing each member and therefore contribute equally to the average. Reflections introduce additional fixed-coloring cases when they are also allowed.

When the conditions change

For rotations sum over shifts or group by divisors. Modular division requires the group size to be invertible or an exact-integer/larger-modulus workaround. CF 711D is a cycle-counting transfer where each functional-graph cycle has forbidden orientations.

Try one change

For length four, is AABB equivalent to ABAB under rotation?

Show the reasoning

No. In AABB some adjacent beads share a color, while in ABAB none do; rotation preserves adjacency.
