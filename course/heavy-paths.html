Trees and paths · 6 of 11

Break a path into logarithmically many array pieces

A value changes at one station. Find the maximum on a route between any two stations of a fixed tree. A single preorder interval need not describe that route.

Work through the example

  1. At each vertex mark the edge to its largest child as heavy. Give every heavy chain consecutive array positions from its head down.
  2. While the two endpoints have different chain heads, query from the deeper head to its endpoint and move that endpoint to the head’s parent.
  3. Once the heads match, query one interval inside that chain. For an edge-valued path store each edge at its deeper vertex and omit the LCA slot.

Why it works

Crossing a light edge upwards at least doubles the subtree size, so a path crosses O(log n) light edges. Maximum is order independent. With strings or function composition, keep one accumulator per end and orient each interval.

When the conditions change

O(log² n) per general segment-tree path query, O(log n) per point update. Heavy-light also mixes subtree assignments with root-path clearing, as in CF 343D. CF 1709E additionally needs reasoning about XOR collisions across branches.

Try one change

Why climb the deeper chain head, even if the other endpoint itself is deeper?

Show the reasoning

The head depth tells which entire chain segment can be removed without crossing the LCA.
