Spend each exponent on at most one number

One operation spends exponent k once, costs 2ᵏ, and right-shifts either of two integers by k bits. Reach equality as cheaply as possible. A cheap shift picked for the left number disappears from the right number’s menu.

Work through the example

  1. Take x=12 and y=6. Shift x once by 1. They meet at 6 for cost 2. The sequence of operations only matters through the total shift assigned to each side.
  2. For x=4 and y=7, reaching 1 on each side would need a total of 2 on each. Each would need exponent 2 because exponent 1 cannot be repeated. That meeting point is impossible.
  3. Both can still meet at zero. Spend exponent 3 on one and exponents 1 and 2 on the other. Each side shifts three places and the total cost is 14.

Why it works

Precompute a minimum-cost table over the pair of accumulated shifts. For each k, write a fresh layer for skipping k, assigning it to x, or assigning it to y. Cap each axis at the maximum bit length B. A capped coordinate means that every possible input on that side becomes zero.

When the conditions change

Also update the symmetric coordinate. Exponents 1 through B suffice for the contest bound B=57 for inputs up to 10¹⁷: exponents 1…11 sum to 66 and 12…16 sum to 70. They already send both numbers to zero for less than 2ᴮ. One can use B=60 and a wide cost type. Precompute in O(B³). For fast queries use suffix minima for the zero meeting and intersect the two descending lists of positive ancestors in O(B).

def shift_tables(bits=60, exponents=None):
    """Capped pair table and its suffix minima. Production bounds use bits=60."""
    exact = {(0, 0): 0}
    for k in (range(1, bits + 1) if exponents is None else exponents):
        following = exact.copy()
        for (a, b), cost in exact.items():
            for cell in ((min(bits, a+k), b), (a, min(bits, b+k))):
                following[cell] = min(following.get(cell, float("inf")), cost + (1 << k))
        exact = following
    suffix = [[exact.get((i,j), float("inf")) for j in range(bits+1)] for i in range(bits+1)]
    for i in range(bits, -1, -1):
        for j in range(bits, -1, -1):
            if i < bits:
                suffix[i][j] = min(suffix[i][j], suffix[i+1][j])
            if j < bits:
                suffix[i][j] = min(suffix[i][j], suffix[i][j+1])
    return exact, suffix


def equalization_cost(x, y, tables):
    exact, suffix = tables
    result = suffix[x.bit_length()][y.bit_length()]
    a = b = 0
    while x and y:
        if x == y:
            result = min(result, exact.get((a, b), float("inf")))
            x >>= 1
            y >>= 1
            a += 1
            b += 1
        elif x > y:
            x >>= 1
            a += 1
        else:
            y >>= 1
            b += 1
    return result
