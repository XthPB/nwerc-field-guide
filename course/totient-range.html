Maintain a product and its prime support under range updates

You maintain an array of positive integers. One operation multiplies every entry in a range by x. Another asks for Euler’s totient of the product in that range, reduced modulo the given prime. The totient counts positive integers up to the product that are coprime with it.

Work through the example

  1. For [6,10], the product is 60 and the distinct primes are {2,3,5}. Euler's formula gives 60·½·⅔·⅘=16. A prime appearing twice still supplies one correction factor.
  2. Store the product modulo M and a bitmask of present primes in each segment-tree node. Merge by multiplication and bitwise OR. A multiplier x on length L multiplies the stored product by xᴸ and ORs in the factors of x.
  3. Compose lazy multipliers by multiplication modulo M and compose their masks by OR. After querying an interval, apply (p−1)/p once for every set bit in the resulting mask.

Why it works

The product supplies all prime powers together. Euler's formula only needs to know which distinct primes divide it, so the mask is sufficient for every query and every future multiplication.

When the conditions change

The contest bounds all initial values and multipliers by 300, so 62 primes fit in a 64-bit mask and all are invertible modulo 10⁹+7. Use a wide unsigned shift. Range operations touch O(log n) canonical nodes, with modular powers costing up to another O(log n) factor.

What if updates divide by primes?

Show the reasoning

A prime can disappear entirely from a segment. OR alone cannot detect that event, so the lazy summary must change.
