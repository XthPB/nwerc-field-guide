Turn distinct assignments into units of flow

Candidates A and B can use stations {1,2} and {1}. Can both be assigned? Giving station 1 to A first looks like a dead end.

Work through the example

  1. Start with A→1. Try B→1. Station 1 is full.
  2. Follow the reverse edge 1→A, then the unused edge A→2. Flip that alternating path.
  3. B uses 1 and A uses 2. Source-to-candidate and station-to-sink capacities of one enforce uniqueness.

Why it works

An augmenting path increases a feasible matching by one. When no such path exists, a residual cut certifies maximum flow. Vertex-disjoint paths use split vertices with a capacity on the internal edge; capacities larger than one model quotas.

When the conditions change

Bipartite matching with Hopcroft–Karp costs O(E√V). Dinic handles general capacities. For assignment costs use Hungarian on a dense square matrix or min-cost flow when capacities or allowed arcs matter. CF 510E asks for degree two, so source/sink capacities change.

Try one change

Why are reverse edges necessary even if all original directions point forward?

Show the reasoning

They permit an earlier assignment to be withdrawn when that is the only way to serve someone else.
