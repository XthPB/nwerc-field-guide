Repair a broken deadline by removing one job

Each job gives one point if you finish it by its deadline and nothing otherwise. You can run one job at a time and choose which to skip. With (duration,deadline) pairs (3,3), (2,4) and (1,4), find the greatest number of points.

Work through the example

  1. After the first job the total duration is 3. Adding the second gives 5 with deadline 4.
  2. Remove the longest job, duration 3. One job survives using only 2 time units.
  3. Adding the final job gives duration 3. Two jobs fit and one time unit is still free.

Why it works

For each prefix, the heap keeps a maximum feasible count with the smallest possible total duration for that count. Removing one job suffices after an insertion because removing the new job alone would restore feasibility. Removing the longest releases at least as much time.

When the conditions change

Use a max-heap and sort by deadline in O(n log n). Different rewards per accepted job change the objective. A related heap repair buys cheaply and may undo a previous transaction in CF 865D. CSES Tasks and Deadlines instead illustrates the adjacent-exchange proof for minimizing total completion time.

Try one change

Why process by deadline rather than by reward or input order?

Show the reasoning

Feasibility of the current prefix is expressed by just its total and the current deadline. A job with an earlier deadline arriving later would break that invariant.
