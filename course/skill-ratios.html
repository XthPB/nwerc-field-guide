Rank improvements to a product by their marginal ratios

You start with positive skill values. Each offer can set one skill to a specified value, add to it, or multiply it. You may use only a given number of offers, each at most once. Choose the offers and their execution order to maximize the product of your final skill values.

Work through the example

  1. A skill starts at 4. Assigning 10 is worth an additive improvement of 6. Adding 4 is the next improvement. The cumulative values 4,10,14 give successive score ratios 10/4 and 14/10. A multiplier of 2 on another skill has ratio 2. With two purchases, choose the assignment and the multiplier.
  2. Per skill, keep only the largest profitable assignment. Treat its excess over the starting value as one addition, sort all positive additions decreasingly, and compute ratios against the running total. Each multiplier already has a fixed ratio.
  3. Merge all ratios across skills and pick the largest allowed gains. Preserve prefix order on ties within a skill. To output real operations, execute selected assignments first, selected ordinary additions next and multiplications last.

Why it works

For any fixed number of additive improvements on a skill, the largest increments give its largest attainable value. Their ratios decrease because the numerator increment shrinks while the current value grows. A global prefix of the best marginal ratios therefore respects the local prerequisites. Multiplying those ratios gives exactly the final product divided by the initial product.

ρj=(vj−1+Δj)/vj−1\rho_j=(v_{j-1}+\Delta_j)/v_{j-1}ρj​=(vj−1​+Δj​)/vj−1​

Here v is the current skill value and Δ is the next additive gain.

When the conditions change

O(n log n) time for n offers. Compare a/b and c/d by wide cross products. Ignore gains at most one when the budget is an upper bound. The assignment keeps its original ID even though selection treated it as a difference.

Try one change

What fails if a skill starts at zero?

Show the reasoning

The initial product and its gain ratios can be undefined. The reduction needs a separate way to make every zero skill positive.

From idea to code

Keep the operation ID with each rational marginal gain. Use a wide cross product in the comparator and sort the chosen IDs by operation type for execution.
