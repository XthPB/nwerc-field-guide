Test choices before committing an entire weight group

A triangle has three edges of weight 1. Which belong to every minimum spanning tree? Processing equal edges one at a time may make the first chosen edge look special.

Work through the example

  1. Before weight 1, all three vertices are separate components. The candidate graph for this weight is the entire triangle.
  2. Its three edges lie on one equal-cost cycle. Every edge can appear and each can be omitted by choosing the other two.
  3. Only after classifying that weight group union all candidates in the main DSU. Higher weights then see the correct contracted graph.

Why it works

Contract strictly lighter connectivity. Inside the temporary multigraph, a bridge is forced and a non-bridge joining different components is optional. An edge whose ends were already connected by lighter weights cannot enter an MST.

When the conditions change

Sort in O(E log E), with linear total bridge work after local compression. For forced inclusion of one non-tree edge, insert it and remove the heaviest edge on its tree path. For a forced set, test for cycles independently within equal-weight groups.

Try one change

Two equal parallel edges survive contraction. Are they both forced?

Show the reasoning

No. Either edge connects the same two components at the same cost.
