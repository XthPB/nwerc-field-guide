Graphs, connectivity and flow · 4 of 27

Find vertices that every shortest route must visit

Trains follow positively weighted connections from one fixed source. Suppose one station closes. Which destinations lose every route that used to attain their shortest travel time, even if a slower detour still exists? The contest asks which permitted closure affects the most destinations.

Work through the example

  1. In the shortest-route graph S→A→C, S→B→C, C→D, station C is compulsory for D. Neither A nor B is compulsory for C.
  2. Run Dijkstra. Keep an arc u→v precisely when dist[u]+w=dist[v]. With positive weights all these arcs go forward in increasing distance. Insert each reachable vertex v into a new tree whose parent is the LCA of all its shortest-route predecessors.
  3. In the example, the LCA of A and B is S. Attach C to S and D to C. Count subtree sizes. Removing C affects its entire subtree, including C itself. Maximize this size over permitted stations.

Why it works

Every route to v ends with one of its predecessor arcs. The vertices compulsory for v before that final step are exactly the intersection of the compulsory ancestor chains of its predecessors. In a rooted tree that intersection is the ancestor chain ending at their LCA.

idom⁡(v)=LCA⁡{u: d(u)+w(u,v)=d(v)}\operatorname{idom}(v)=\operatorname{LCA}\{u:\ d(u)+w(u,v)=d(v)\}idom(v)=LCA{u: d(u)+w(u,v)=d(v)}

The LCA is taken in the dominator tree built so far, over incoming shortest-route predecessors.

When the conditions change

O((n+m) log n) time with a heap and online binary lifting. Exclude unreachable vertices. Positive weights give the needed DAG. Zero-weight cycles require a separate dominator treatment.

Try one change

What if one alternative route takes one minute longer?

Show the reasoning

It is absent from the shortest-route DAG. A station can be compulsory for optimal routes even when a slower detour survives.

From idea to code

Use Dijkstra for the distance order. Initialize one row of a binary-lifting table as each dominator-tree vertex is inserted and fold its predecessors with LCA.
