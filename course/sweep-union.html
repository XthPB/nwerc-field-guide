Range queries and sweeps · 8 of 12

Sweep slabs and maintain covered length with multiplicity

Find the union area of [0,2]×[0,2] and [1,3]×[1,3]. Adding individual areas counts their common square twice.

Work through the example

  1. Between x=0 and x=1 the covered y length is 2. Between x=1 and x=2 the intervals overlap and cover length 3.
  2. Between x=2 and x=3 the covered length falls to 2. The total is 2+3+2=7.
  3. Sort x-events. Before applying events at x, charge the horizontal distance since the last x times the old covered y length.

Why it works

Compress y endpoints and store cover count plus covered length for each elementary-interval segment tree node. A positive cover count fills the node’s whole physical length. At zero cover count, sum its children.

When the conditions change

O(n log n). A coordinate gap contributes its real length, not one compressed slot. For perimeter or maximum overlap the event tie rules and the information stored at the root change.

Try one change

Two identical rectangles are inserted and one is removed. What would a Boolean covered flag forget?

Show the reasoning

One rectangle still covers the whole region. A multiplicity distinguishes one removal from clearing the region.
