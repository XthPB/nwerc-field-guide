Discard an interior point with a three-point turn

Find the boundary of {(0,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,2),(0,2)}. The middle point (1,1) lies inside, but sorting by x alone cannot detect that.

Work through the example

  1. Sort and deduplicate points lexicographically. On the lower walk, when a new point makes the last turn clockwise, the middle point cannot be a lower-hull corner.
  2. Pop while the desired turn condition fails, then append. Build the upper walk on the reversed order.
  3. Join the two chains without doubling their endpoints. For a fully collinear set decide whether the requested output includes all boundary points or only the two extremes.

Why it works

Each pop replaces a two-edge detour by a supporting chord. Each point is appended and popped at most once per walk. For strict containment of one convex set in another, distinguish touching an edge from lying inside.

When the conditions change

O(n log n) including sorting. Rotating calipers uses a consistent cyclic hull; its antipodal pointer moves only forward as the supporting edge turns. Deduplicate before treating very small hulls separately.
