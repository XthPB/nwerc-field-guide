Read all palindromic radii in linear time

Every palindrome has a center. Expanding independently from every center of “aaaaa” repeats many comparisons. What can symmetry save?

Work through the example

  1. At its middle character the palindrome extends to both ends. At nearby centers, their mirror across that middle supplies an already known radius, limited by the right boundary.
  2. Only compare new characters when the mirrored radius reaches that boundary. Each successful extra comparison pushes the global right boundary outward.
  3. Compute odd and even centers separately or interleave separators consistently. Count palindromic occurrences by adding the number of valid radii at each center.

Why it works

The mirror is exact inside the already verified palindrome. Beyond its boundary you have learned nothing. CF 17E uses center counts to count disjoint pairs and subtracts from all pairs to obtain intersecting ones.

When the conditions change

Manacher runs in O(n). A palindromic tree stores distinct palindrome strings and suffix links; it suits online suffix information and series-link partition DP. CF 835D adds a recursive property of palindrome halves.
