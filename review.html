Designing the state

You want a cheapest schedule. Two ways reach the same time, but only the more expensive one still has its one-use cancellation coupon. Is it safe to keep only the cheaper schedule?

Keep one minimum per coupon state. An expensive prefix with the unused coupon can save more later. Merge only after fixing future eligibility.

A tour table has one entry per subset of 20 vertices and per possible last vertex. Could you store an eight-byte score and an eight-byte parent for each entry in 256 MiB?

The score table alone is 160 MiB. Two such tables cost 320 MiB. Compact the parent, store only reachable endpoints, roll compatible layers, or reconstruct in another pass.

Multidimensional states, from 2D to 4D

Items have known widths and must each go into one of three bags. After processing a prefix you know its total width and the loads of bags one and two. When can you infer the third load?

Only when the processed total is known and every processed item has been assigned exactly once. If skipping is allowed, track the total assigned as well or choose a different state.

Three changing strings must fit into distinct positions in one host, preserving the order within each string. You remove the last character x from one string and append y in its place. Which previously computed entries can you reuse?

Overwrite the newly exposed whole face, in dependency order, with the transitions for the new character. Earlier positions in the host dominate later positions for the same triple.

Knapsack and subset sum

You have six interchangeable tickets worth 2 apiece. You want to count the ways to make exact totals. Would grouping the tickets into bundles of one, two and three and counting one-use bundle subsets preserve those counts?

No. Three tickets are represented by {3} and by {1,2}, although both represent one original choice. Count a bounded type using an old-row sum along its residue class.

Meet in the middle

From 40 large signed integers, choose exactly 12 with sum between A and B. You plan to enumerate choices separately for the first and last 20 numbers. What must you store to join them correctly?

Bucket each half’s subset sums by size. For a left sum x with c items, use two binary searches in the sorted right bucket 12−c for [A−x,B−x]. Keep duplicates for counting.

Count corner-to-corner grid paths with a target XOR, moving right and down. You enumerate both halves up to the same diagonal cell. Why might the count fail only when that cell is nonzero?

Check whether both path halves include their common cell. If they do, join by right = target XOR left XOR cell and restrict the map to the same cell.

Sequences, scheduling and sweeps

In an increasing subsequence problem, each chosen value earns its own reward. A larger tail may have earned 50 points while a smaller tail earned 1. Can you keep only the smallest tail for each length when maximizing the score?

Length alone loses profit. Keep the best reward by ending rank and query the prefix of strictly smaller ranks. Recover the source index if a witness is needed.

Split people with known skills into groups, charging each group its largest skill minus its smallest. As you sweep the skills in order, what tells you how much cost accumulates before the next skill?

The cost incurred across the next coordinate gap is exactly the gap times the number open. Every completed span is the sum of the gaps it covers. Identities matter only through multiplicities of the next placement.

Strings and alignment

You count cuts of a string into palindromic blocks. A million identical letters make every suffix a possible final block. Can you afford to visit every palindromic suffix at every endpoint?

It makes Θ(n²) visits over all prefixes. Group suffix lengths with equal successive differences using series links and aggregate their contribution.

Given two permutations of the same n symbols, find their longest common subsequence. How can knowing that each symbol appears once save you from an n-by-n table?

Replace symbols in the first sequence by their positions in the second. Their strict LIS is the LCS. With repeats, list all matching positions in descending order within each first-string position.

Interval and partition DP

In a shrinking row of colors, one move removes consecutive items of one color. A move now earns the square of the number removed. What extra information matters when deciding whether two separated runs should merge?

Matching a distant block changes the eventual group size and thus its reward. Give the interval a third coordinate for attached equal blocks before attempting any speedup.

You want to count valid orders of deletion. Once you choose the last item removed from an interval, the left and right sides can be solved independently. Does multiplying their order counts count every full order?

Only if the relative schedule between sides is fixed. If L and R moves can interleave arbitrarily, multiply by choose(L+R,L) as well.

Bitmasks and subset transforms

For each subset S of twenty items, you need the sum of values assigned to all subsets contained in S. Which property of the aggregation lets twenty sweeps over the masks produce every answer?

When each answer aggregates a value belonging to the submask itself. If a transition instead combines arbitrary DP values for both the submask and its complement inside M, the plain SOS transform does not supply it.

Read the lesson · Sum over submasks

A courier must visit each city before returning to the depot. All travel costs are nonnegative. If two partial routes have visited the same cities in different orders, can the DP safely forget their endpoints?

No. Two orders of that set can end in different cities whose next-edge costs differ. Nonnegativity leaves that dependence intact.

Grid and lattice paths, tilings and frontiers

Place a single connected loop on a board with 6 rows and 100,000 columns. As you process a strip at a time, which boundary should you expose, and what do you need to remember about the open ends?

Sweep along the long side. Preserve occupancy, degree obligations and how frontier ends are connected across the six-cell width. Enforce the rule about premature loops.

Count right-and-down grid paths that avoid several blocked cells. If a path can visit two blocked cells, what happens when you subtract the count through each blocked cell separately?

A path visiting two obstacles would be subtracted twice. Subtract ways whose first obstacle is the earlier one, using the already-corrected count for each predecessor.

Dynamic programming on graphs

Find a cheapest connected subgraph joining seven required terminals in a graph of 100,000 vertices. If a DP must enumerate subsets, which objects belong in those subsets?

Mask the seven terminals. Keep a graph vertex as the meeting point. Merge complementary terminal masks there and run shortest-path closure for the merged mask.

Dynamic programming on trees

For every possible root of a tree you want a product of independent branch contributions modulo 12. When sending an answer back to one child, how do you combine all the other branches if its value has no modular inverse?

Use prefix and suffix products over neighbours. An individual factor may have no inverse modulo 12, even when it is nonzero.

You want the largest vertex-weight sum of a simple path in a tree. A child reports a path worth 18 that joins two branches beneath it. Can the parent safely extend that path across the parent edge?

Passing the current vertex toward its parent consumes another direction. The parent can extend only an open path with one child arm. A two-arm path is complete.

Geometry inside a DP

Nest envelopes with strictly increasing width and height. Two envelopes of equal width have different heights. Which ordering or update rule prevents the algorithm from putting one inside the other?

Sort equal widths by descending height for length-only LIS, or query their whole batch before submitting any updates in weighted DP.

Split a polygon into triangles using diagonals that stay inside it. In a convex polygon every diagonal between nonneighboring vertices is valid. What must you check before reusing that recurrence on a concave polygon?

The boundary chord and proposed incident triangle must be legal and lie inside. Boundary order alone no longer certifies this.

Digit DP, binary bounds and carries

Count integers from 1 to N whose usual decimal representation contains a zero. If you give every integer the same width, how do you keep the padding in 007 from changing whether 7 qualifies?

Keep started and seenRealZero flags. Padding before the first nonzero digit leaves both unchanged. Handle the actual integer zero separately if it enters the requested interval.

Read the lesson · Digit DP on a numerical bound

You want the sum of all integers up to N whose digits meet a rule. As you append the next digit, what must a state retain besides the number of qualifying prefixes?

A count and a sum. Appending digit d in base b sends (count,sum) to (count,b·sum+d·count).

Probability, expectations and games

One trial costs a turn. It succeeds with probability p greater than zero and otherwise returns to its starting state. Why does an ordinary memoized recursion get stuck when asked for the expected time to succeed?

The recurrence contains its own unknown. Isolate the self-loop and divide by the probability of leaving. If p=0, success is unreachable.

Two impartial games each have Grundy number 3. Players take turns changing exactly one game, and whoever has no move loses. Who wins from the combined starting position?

The starting XOR is 3 XOR 3 = 0, a losing position under finite normal play. The fact that either component alone is winning does not answer the sum.

Randomization and DP

You seek a simple path by randomly coloring vertices and looking for a path using different colors. Two trials found nothing and the third produced a candidate. Do those earlier failures affect whether the candidate itself is valid?

Every successful witness can be checked deterministically. The error is one-sided and concerns failing to find any witness when one exists.

Range queries inside DP

Choose a chain of points with both coordinates strictly increasing. You sort by x and query earlier y values, but two points with the same x enter the same chain. How can you prevent that?

Query all points of the same x against the previous groups, then insert their results. Use a y prefix ending one rank below the queried point.

Advanced range queries inside DP

A query on an interval asks for both its best score and the number of positions attaining it. Why can merging two overlapping blocks count too many winners even when their best score is correct?

A shared maximizing predecessor contributes twice. Query disjoint blocks or use an idempotent operation when overlap is unavoidable.

Optimizing transitions

You split an array into groups and pay the square of each group sum. A fast method used a proof assuming nonnegative entries. Which part of the optimization must be checked again if entries can be negative?

The four-term proof contains a product of two prefix increments. With mixed signs it can be negative. Return to the baseline or prove a different property.

Algebraic and counting DP

To recover coefficients from a binary XOR transform you usually divide by 2ᵇ. Your required modulus is even. What extra precision would let you do an ordinary exact division at the end?

For integer forward convolutions/powers, lift the whole computation to modulus 2ᵇp. The canonical residue after the second transform is divisible by 2ᵇ, yielding the desired residue modulo p.

In a directed graph with signed edge costs you need the cheapest walk using exactly t edges. When multiplying transition matrices, how should you combine costs, compare routes, and represent a zero-edge walk?

Use min-plus multiplication, zero diagonal and ∞ off-diagonal for identity. Finite-length walks are well defined even if some edge costs are negative.

Combining and compressing techniques

A subset-sum instance contains 240,000 positive items, many copies of the same small weights. You only need reachability. How can you use the multiplicities to avoid one full bitset shift per item?

Multiplicity carrying preserves feasibility while leaving at most two copies of any weight. The total sum then bounds the number of distinct remaining shifts by O(√S).

Query strictly descendant lines first. A self-line creates a forbidden self-transition and can contaminate later ancestors.

Newer Codeforces ideas

You may cut tree edges so each resulting component’s XOR is one of six specified values. A subtree can still have one component open to its parent. What limits that open component’s possible XOR values despite 30-bit inputs?

Completed-component XORs belong to the binary span of the six allowed values, of dimension at most six. Track closed XOR in that basis; the open XOR follows from the fixed subtree XOR.

Count grid paths from one corner to the opposite corner. Two cells have the same number of complete paths through them. Does that prove that every path visiting either cell also visits the other?

Equality of counts is insufficient. In a diamond the two branch cells each see one path, but the paths differ. Test mutual dominance and postdominance or an equivalent exact path-set criterion.
