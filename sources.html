Sources and checks

The examples use public contest statements, contest analyses and small independently computed instances. The index records problem IDs, so two divisions can contain the same underlying task.

How the examples were selected

The CHipCie organizer archive lists 31 editions under NWERC from 1995 through 2025. I surveyed the available jury slides or problem booklets for all 31, using OCR on two early booklets (1995 and 1997). A statement-derived row is my reduction. Jury-slide rows paraphrase the cited idea. The archive also has earlier WERC and ERC predecessor contests under different labels. NWERC archive

The BAPC survey covers eleven jury decks, 2016–2026. Selected SWERC, GCPC, North Central North America, Asia Yokohama and World Finals materials extend the regional sample. The World Finals document describes itself as the authors’ unofficial solution sketches hosted by ICPC. BAPC archive · Problem-by-problem sources

The 2022 and 2023 NWERC decks were examined privately. Their statements, letters, names, year-to-technique mappings and direct solution links are omitted from the reader. BAPC and North Central NA entries from those years are separate contests.

The regular Codeforces API includes 2,315 rated DP-tagged problem IDs between 800 and 3000 in the snapshot. The full index contains 2322 IDs including additional appearances or relevant problems under other tags. 147 IDs have a problem-specific note and lesson. Other entries link to template candidates based on their official tags or identifiable operations in a public implementation. Those links describe starting points. Codeforces problemset API

When available, the index also links to an independent author’s source file at a fixed revision. An operation found in code can provide supporting material without proving it is the main solution. EndlessCheng’s implementations · DionysiosB’s implementations

The newer selection runs from 2025-11-14 to 2026-10-14. The local index also allows filtering all rated entries by the actual year of the round. Newer derivations

Template checks

The companion adds ten general C++17 implementations. The original digit-DP snippet has a corrected negative-bound return in its public copy. The original notes and companion pages identify their assumptions and dependencies.

The ten new C++17 building blocks passed 28694 comparisons and assertions, including brute-force tours, independent sets, interval order statistics, absolute-error fits, obstacles and turn counts, decimal bounds, substring automata, and rerooting under prime and composite moduli.

4709 independent comparisons across 10 additional derivations. The comparisons include connected grid frontiers, deletion-order interleavings, stack ordering and squared-distance rerooting.
