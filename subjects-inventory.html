What the counts tell you

Family counts group same-start entries with a shared normalized title after applying the rating and tag filters. A family is outside the earlier DP draw if none of its entries appeared in that 1,500-problem sample. A new family can still use a familiar idea.

Published implementations are matched by exact contest and problem ID to a source file at a fixed Git revision. Across 800–3000 there are 7,070 such links, including 1,729 at 2000–3000. These links let readers inspect a solution. Guide mappings count individual problems with an authored lesson or technique note. The two columns can overlap.
