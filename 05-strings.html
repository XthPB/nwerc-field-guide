Test your recognition
A million identical letters make every suffix palindromic. Is one visit per palindromic suffix efficient?

It makes Θ(n²) visits over all prefixes. Group suffix lengths with equal successive differences using series links and aggregate their contribution.

The two strings are permutations of the same n symbols. How can you avoid n² LCS cells?

Replace symbols in the first sequence by their positions in the second. Their strict LIS is the LCS. With repeats, list all matching positions in descending order within each first-string position.
