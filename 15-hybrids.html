Combining and compressing techniques

Take a familiar table and change how its inputs arrive, repeat or interact. The goal is to notice which part stays ordinary and which part needs a new argument.

Test your recognition
A 240000-item subset sum has tiny repeated positive weights. What extra structure is available beyond a bitset?

Multiplicity carrying preserves feasibility while leaving at most two copies of any weight. The total sum then bounds the number of distinct remaining shifts by O(√S).

A DP on descendants queries a line minimum. Should a node insert its own line before or after querying?

Query strictly descendant lines first. A self-line creates a forbidden self-transition and can contaminate later ancestors.
