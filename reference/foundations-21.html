Suffix structures and palindrome algorithms

Sort every suffix once

A suffix array lists starting positions in lexicographic order. Suffixes sharing a prefix form a contiguous interval. The LCP array stores the common-prefix length of adjacent suffixes; the common prefix of any two suffixes is the minimum LCP between their ranks.

For banana, sorted suffixes are a, ana, anana, banana, na, nana. The adjacent LCP values are 1,3,0,0,2 under a between-neighbours convention. A repeated substring appears as a shared prefix of adjacent suffixes.

Doubling construction

Initially rank characters. At step k, compare suffixes by the pair (rank[i],rank[i+2^k]), using a sentinel rank beyond the end. Sort pairs and assign new ranks. After O(log n) rounds, ranks describe entire suffixes.

Ordinary comparison sorting each round gives O(n log² n); radix/counting sorting ranks gives O(n log n). SA-IS gives linear time with a more complex implementation and is available in established libraries. Learn the doubling proof before treating a linear constructor as a black box.

Kasai's LCP reuse

Process suffix starts in original order. If suffix i and its predecessor in suffix-array order share h characters, moving to i+1 reduces a useful lower bound by at most one. Reuse h−1 and extend. This gives O(n) LCP construction after the suffix array.

Karaoke Compression can group adjacent suffixes with LCP≥L for each fixed L, collect their original positions, and greedily count non-overlapping occurrences. Repeating every length still costs substantial work: the suffix array alone does not make the whole problem O(n log n).

Suffix automaton

A suffix automaton compresses all substrings into states with the same end-position behaviour. Each state has a maximum length and suffix link; it represents substring lengths from len[link]+1 through len[state]. Thus the number of distinct substrings is the sum of those differences.

Construction is linear for a fixed alphabet, but cloning states is the conceptual hurdle: when a new transition would mix different length ranges, clone the transition behaviour and split the represented class. Occurrence counts are propagated through suffix links in decreasing length order.

Palindromes: use the symmetry directly

Manacher's algorithm computes odd and even palindrome radii in O(n). The current rightmost palindrome reflects an initial radius for positions inside it; only extensions beyond its boundary require new character comparisons. Odd and even centres use different indexing formulas, so test empty, one-character, even-length and all-equal strings.

An eertree (palindromic tree) stores distinct palindromic substrings with suffix links. It is useful for online palindrome counting and palindrome-partition DP. It is broader preparation rather than a frequent requirement established by this archive.

Checkpoint: how do you find the longest repeated substring with a suffix array?

Take the maximum adjacent LCP. Any two suffixes sharing a prefix have all suffixes between them sharing at least that prefix, so an adjacent pair witnesses the optimum. Non-overlap constraints need additional reasoning.
