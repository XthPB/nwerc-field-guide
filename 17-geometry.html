Geometry inside a DP

Decide what the coordinates do. They can order choices, price movement, separate regions, or identify two unfinished endpoints. Only then choose which coordinates belong in the table.

  • Replace strict geometric containment with a chainNest envelopes of sizes (2,3), (2,4) and (3,5). Both dimensions must grow strictly. What is the longest nesting?
  • Turn a reachability cone into coordinate inequalitiesWhich events at different times and positions can one walker attend at unit speed?
  • Absolute distance can turn all predecessors into two sweepsAt every point i on a line, buy from the source minimizing its starting price plus travel distance to i.
  • Compute the cheapest Manhattan delivery to every grid cellEach source cell has a starting price. Delivering one square vertically or horizontally costs one. How do you find the cheapest delivered price at every cell of an unobstructed rectangle?
  • Geometry can determine the price of a combinatorial moveA courier can collect one or two points per round trip. With squared-distance prices, when is a paired trip cheaper?
  • Choose the triangle incident to a boundary chordTriangulate a convex polygon while keeping the most expensive triangle as cheap as possible.
  • Two monotone chains replace a visited setDraw a closed tour through points in increasing x along one side and decreasing x on the way back. Can you avoid a visited-set table?
  • Four directional path tables can meet around a crossingTwo walkers cross a grid along different monotone directions. How can you evaluate the best routes around their meeting?

Every triangulation contains exactly one triangle touching this boundary side. Its third vertex is either 2 or 3.

Try third vertex 2

This draws diagonal 0–2, of length √20. The two triangle perimeters together contain it twice.

Try third vertex 3

This draws diagonal 1–3, of length √17. Boundary length is identical, so this choice minimizes total triangle perimeter.

Test your recognition
Two envelopes have the same width but different heights. How should a strict nesting sweep handle them?

Sort equal widths by descending height for length-only LIS, or query their whole batch before submitting any updates in weighted DP.

What part of the convex-polygon triangulation recurrence requires extra work for a concave polygon?

The boundary chord and proposed incident triangle must be legal and lie inside. Boundary order alone no longer certifies this.
